IDs are stable anchors, not published numbers; preserve the seven baseline
tables and three notes. The displayed order and numbering may change.
Keep the evidence needed to understand each inference in prose, the
comparable observations in tables, and verification details in notes or
the appendix. The source-bearing IDs resolve through the
[historical model](ARGUMENT_CYCLE13.md); all anchors resolve in the
[paragraph plan](PARAGRAPH_PLAN.md).
