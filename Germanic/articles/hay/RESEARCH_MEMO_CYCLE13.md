# Comparative implementation memorandum

Research branch `research/hay-article`, base `9cd14b07`. The question is
whether the earliest Anglian evidence requires **replacement of the
nucleus**, even if regular survival of the glide is rejected. An
independently diagnostic protected nucleus in those forms, or evidence
excluding both inherited-nucleus paths, would undermine the alternative.
This is a lexical and publication inquiry, not a new SC047 verdict or
firing census. The frozen orders and predictions remain those verified
in the [previous source audit](RESEARCH_MEMO_CYCLE12.md).

## New source finding: opposite directions in Ball and Stiles

Checked the local original, *Anglia* 101: 5–28, §§4.2–4.3,
pp. 15–19 (PDF sheets 11–15); note 18 starts on p. 18 and
continues on p. 19. The paper opens with Leiden *goelu*, not
an independently observed early back-mutated *geolu*.

| Word | Reconstructed histories in their analysis | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|
| ‘yellow’ | Inflected *gelw-* (including the weak declension) > *geolw-* by breaking before *lw*. Direct *gelu* escapes that environment; the Anglian form can later undergo back-mutation. In West Saxon they expect palatal diphthongization of the unlevelled direct form (*gielu*) but infer prehistoric regularization in favour of *geolw-*, probably before i-mutation. | Monophthongal forms occur in the early direct singular, not throughout the paradigm. The expected West Saxon alternation is reconstructed, not witnessed. |
| ‘meal, flour’ | Direct *melu* escapes breaking and can later give *meolu* by back-mutation; inflected *melw-* regularly breaks to *meolw-*. Between these changes, they infer that frequent direct *melu* supplied *e* to new inflected *melw-* forms. These forms in turn reinforced *melu* against the new *meolu* at back-mutation. | Both *melu/melwes* and *meolu/meolwes* are reported. This reverses the direction proposed by Luick and Sievers–Brunner for the monophthongal direct form; the rival explanation and the uncertainty of breaking matter. |
| ‘fat’ | By parallel reasoning, unbroken direct vocalism can spread back into *rw*-bearing inflected forms after breaking and before back-mutation. | Inflected *smer(u)w-* is frequent in the *Leechdoms* and occurs elsewhere; the early Épinal–Érfurt dative spellings are their evidence for an unbroken inflected stem. The analogy date is their inference. |
| ‘tar’ | Unlike ‘meal’ and ‘fat’, the reported distribution resembles ‘yellow’: early *teru/tero*, otherwise *teoru/teorw-*, no *terw-*. | Note 18 allows transmission as an explanation and points to *teorwena*, a reported genitive plural, as a possible sign of different usage. Neither their semantics nor a direction of levelling is proved. |

The comparison supplies evidence that the relative availability of stem
alternants is a real historical question, not that any particular
grammatical cell of ‘hay’ won. The *wa*-stems supply written alternants
in inflected forms; ‘hay’ supplies a reconstructed protected *ja*-stem
and later *hege* without diagnostic vocalism. Neither the 47 direct
tokens among 51 case-assigned occurrences nor the absence of a secure
plural measures prehistoric frequencies. Direct survival, extension
of direct vocalism, protected-stem substitution, restoration of /j/,
and loss of *-e* must therefore remain distinct.

## Interpretation and boundaries

For regular survival, the candidate environment is a stressed short
/e/ followed by fully unstressed final /i/ in hiatus after *w*-loss,
before any contraction in the variety concerned. The available controls
motivate a mechanism but do not determine its domain: *glī* tests
identical vowels, *sǣ/ǣ* start with length, *gecēd/strēde* are nonfinal,
and Hogg’s desyllabification examples have back second vowels. No
new change, matched control or experiment is claimed. If final
desyllabification fails, contraction plus glide restoration remains
a motivated, conditional way to retain the origin of the vowel.
If both routes fail, the positive case against required transfer is
weakened; the separate spelling, reconstruction and quantity findings
survive. This is the load-bearing distinction, not a claim that the
two routes are equally likely or that mass-noun semantics chooses one.

No new witness collation, Farman recalibration, model execution or
general acquisition. Dal, Wallenberg and Brooks & Kelly remain with
the project owner. No change to production inputs or metadata.
