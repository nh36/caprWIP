# Hay source handoff

## Cycle-3 edition and manuscript consultations, 24 November 2026

New consultations this cycle (none added to the private library; all are
public-domain editions or open manuscript digitizations, cited directly):

- **Skeat 1887** (Matthew re-edition): HathiTrust full view, record
  `inu.32000000891434`. Pages 59 and 123 image-verified: Ru1 *hóeg* with
  acute at both loci; Li Mt 14:19 printed *heg\** with apparatus
  "\*hehg, *alt. to* heg" — resolving the K–H/Cook/DOE discrepancy.
- **Krapp 1932, ASPR 5** (*The Paris Psalter and the Meters of
  Boethius*): archive.org lending copy `parispsaltermete0000unse`.
  Pages 31, 72–73, 75, 84, 146 verified, including the apparatus
  "146,9,2 hig] hio".
- **Krapp 1932, ASPR 2** (*The Vercelli Book*): archive.org copy
  `vercellibook0002geor`. Page 4 verified (Andreas 36–39).
- **BnF lat. 8824** (the Paris Psalter manuscript): Gallica digitization
  `ark:/12148/btv1b8451636f` (394 canvases, complete). Folios 116r
  (canvas f241), 116v (f242) and 118v (f246) inspected at 2400 px: all
  three *hege* tokens plainly legible, unaccented, facing the Latin
  *faenum* verses.
- **Digital Bodleian, Auct. D. 2. 19** (Rushworth/Macregol): IIIF
  manifest holds only 49 selected folios; the two hay pages are not
  digitized. Recorded as a verification limit.

## Cycle-4 consultations (24 November 2026)

- **Sweet 1885, *Oldest English Texts*** (archive.org
  `oldestenglishtex83sweeuoft`; the complete scan is also held locally at
  `docs/references/sweet_1885_oldest_english_texts.pdf`): Ct. 43 =
  S 1196 (p. 450, *cyniges hei weg*, dated 859) verified, with the ⟨ei⟩
  charter controls *dei* (Ct. 39, 42) and *deg/dege* (Ct. 41, 45).
  *Deimund* occurs in the S 1196 witness-list, whose hand/stage is not a
  secure same-scribe control (Lowe 2001, pp. 76–78).
- **Electronic Sawyer, S 1196**: conventionally dated 859; Lowe (2001,
  pp. 76–78, 85–86) styles the date ?859 and discusses a possible later
  copy/date slip and separately added witnesses. The surviving Christ
  Church single sheet is BL Cotton Augustus ii.16, s. ix med.; this does
  not establish that every textual layer is contemporary.
- **Wright–Wülcker I** (already held): no. XIII "Semi-Saxon Vocabulary"
  col. 547 *Fenum, hei, uel græs*; its exemplar entry in no. X *Foenum,
  hig, oðde gærs*; F.174 ⟨ei⟩ controls *Uia, wei*, *weibreode*.
- **Skeat 1871 (Mark)**: WS-control sweep — *weg* ⟨weg(e)(s)⟩ 31×, never
  ⟨wig/wyg/wieg⟩; Royal 1 A.xiv *hig* 'they' = hī, *hwig/hwyg* = hwī.
- **Boutkan 1995, pp. 212–213** (local
  `Boutkan1995.txt`): MDu *hoye* < \*hauj- vs *houwe* < \*hawi; OHG
  *hewi* ~ *houwi* doublets; chronology discussion.
- **Kuhn 1945, p. 646** (local `kuhn1945.txt`): *oele* = i-umlaut of
  short Latin *o*; basis of the cycle-4 ⟨oe⟩ reassessment.
- **Tamoto 2013** (local `tamoto2013.txt`): full accent census of
  Farman's stint (Mt 1:1–Mk 2:15), committed as `FARMAN_ACCENTS.tsv`
  with generator `extract_farman_accents.py`.
