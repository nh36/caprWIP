### Bidirectional audit requirements

Read from each subsection back to its E/C/H entries, then from C01–09
forward to its article locus. Check separately the two non-implications:
exact spelling is not phonological or grammatical identity, and a
possible donor is not directionally established transfer. No inference
about short vowels may be drawn merely from absence of accent.
