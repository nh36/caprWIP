# Worked change-control example: *heiweg*

**Historical chain.** E01–03/E31 give C01, *he.i* only at the
hiatus stage. E06 is Sweet's edition reading *cyniges hei weg*
with its stated division, not a collation. E07–08 and E26–28
compare the glosses, Kentish *dei*, meadows, *cyninges strete*,
*hēah-* and the documentary concerns. C04 prefers ‘hay-road’
without excluding ‘highway’. With C01 this yields C05, a
conditional exact match in letters. E09–10 and C02 keep open
different composition histories and later orthographic routes.
None of these gives independent length, hiatus or a grammatical
cell. The rest of the [graph](ARGUMENT_CYCLE13.md#dependency-index)
distinguishes corroboration of C10/C09 from necessary premises.

**Counterfactual proposal: new evidence establishes ‘highway’.** Change
E07/C04 after verifying the evidence; withdraw C05 as corroboration for
‘hay’. Preserve E06 and the actual letters, recording the new reading
separately. Reweigh E26–28 only where affected. After acceptance, revise
R3's purpose and the P-char sequence, plus P-abstract, P-motivation,
P-compare-forms and P-conclusion as relevant. Change the comparison to
a screened rival rather than an early hay attestation, and update any
charter apparatus affected. Do not alter E08's *dei* observation, the
secure compounds, the Worcester simplex, Table 1, early Anglian simplex
evidence, Farman, the West Saxon inference, or the positive basis of
C10/C09. The loss of corroboration may weaken the case for C11 but does
not decide or refute H1. Documentary concerns remain and cannot be
removed just by changing the gloss.

**Style-only proposal (not applied):** in P-char-rival, put the candidate
interpretation first, then explain the conditional reference of the two
royal-road expressions. An illustrative sentence is “If both expressions
refer to the same route, *cyninges strete* supports its importance, not the
lexical identification of the first element.” This changes information
order within the sentence, not E26–28. Keep the condition, citation,
counterexample (*communis via* is not a gloss), documentary distinction,
and following phonological question. Assert neither that the route was
royal in both clauses nor that the comparison refutes ‘highway’.
