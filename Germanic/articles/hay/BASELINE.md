The first layer constrains the second, the second the third, and so on.
The order of proof need not be the order of presentation. Historical
revision propagates only along affected dependencies; rhetorical and
stylistic revision cannot silently flow upstream.

Use the [five comparative memoranda](research/comparative-memoranda/) for
editorial method (Laker, Versloot, Ball and Stiles, Ritt, and the synthesis).
Cite original scholarship, not the commissioned analyses. Use the current
section of [UNRESOLVED.md](UNRESOLVED.md) as the open-question list. The
Cycle 8 denial of *w*-loss and obligatory levelling result are withdrawn;
Cycle 9's categorical final contraction is not current; neither normalized
*hīeġ* nor the authentic but unassigned Eadwine ⟨hieg⟩ supplies an early
West Saxon manuscript attestation of the normalized form.

**Scope and decision:** Cycle 14 records an editorial proposal. No new
evidence, historical verdict, calibrated counts, model execution or rule
change; no frozen or production inputs and neither publication file change.
See the [revision protocol](REVISION_PROTOCOL.md) and [charter demonstration](HEIWEG_DEMONSTRATION.md).
