# Q1a: The quantity of the ‘flock’ ending

## Identity and question

Research baseline `5a480e470c19c8877d23b428bad89772f22192d3`; frozen FST comparator `113101ac5f169b589e1a9647cdf24d32cdd4d919`. This is a correction to the research use of SC063 (`OEHighVowelApocope`), not a review of its production verdict. Does ‘flock’ independently require a short final vowel eligible for deletion when the stem has become heavy?

## Morphology and chronology

- Kroonen (2013: 45, *awi-*, *awidja-*) supports short root *awi-* and a dental collective in *-dja-*. This is a stem reconstruction, not a transcription of the English direct ending at HVD. The [EWA *ewit* entry](https://ewa.saw-leipzig.de/articles/34800/en) distinguishes probably masculine *ewit* from neuter ja-stem *ouwiti* and compares Gothic *aweþi*, genitive *aweþjis*. These support a dental collective and a *j*-bearing inflectional analysis, not a direct measurement of the English direct vowel.
- [Kluge](https://archive.org/details/nominalestammbi00kluggoog) (1886: 33 §70, scan sheet 53) groups Gothic, OHG and English under collective *ja*-stems; he expresses uncertainty about the **medial** suffix vowel. [Wilmanns](https://archive.org/details/deutschegrammat02wilm) (1899: 350 §264 and 352 §265.2c, scan sheets 370 and 372) derives OHG *-idi* from extension to neuter *ja*-stems and connects the sheep collective with a *ja*-extension. His Bavarian long-vowel discussion concerns the vowel **before** *d*, not the final *i* of English ‘flock’. Neither passage identifies a short vowel at the English deletion stage.
- Ringe & Taylor’s general light-stem *-i* (2014: 387) gives a comparative reason to test a short-ending alternative, not to apply that analysis to this multisyllabic derivative without first establishing its inherited suffix, weight and direct allomorph. Their contrastive assumption for the longer derived form is explicit on p. 259. They order word-final long-vowel shortening after short high-vowel apocope, or allow the two effects as part of one process (301–302, §6.8.3). Neither formulation supplies a short input to apocope here. Later lowering can produce *-e*; it cannot date earlier stem resolution. The donors and distribution of possible w-bearing renewal remain as in the [attestation dossier](../hiatus-cycle3/FLOCK.md); there is no separate positive evidence for renewal of this ending.

## Diagnosis and disposition

The [corrected R4 receipt](../hiatus-cycle3/chronology-corrected-traces.json.gz) already contains the matching long-final projection as well as the short one. For long *ī*, all three early heavy mechanisms and all three later mechanisms retain the ending and yield *ēde* in canonical, A and B2. With short *i*, the early heavy arms give *ēd*, late arms give *ēde*, and an independently light early nucleus need not feed deletion. These are valid controlled contrasts. **The published long-final history permits either timing relative to HVD.** The short-final result becomes an ordering discriminator only if new evidence independently establishes that input at deletion.

**DEFER** a historical timing inference from ‘flock’; **RETAIN** SC063 and both experimental projections. The chief positive evidence of this comparison is now the source-based short stem hiatus and eventual long stem vowel, not the date of heaviness. Neither a new early shortening nor a production chronology edge is warranted.

`Registry-verdict: SC063=REFORMULATE`
