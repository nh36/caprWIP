# L1b — Nuclear realization and protected labials

The [second tranche](validation-nuclei-v1.tsv) records 24 new or explicitly reused research rows, of which 22 are reserved: 21 executable comparisons or conditional probes and one observation. The previously used ‘friend’ and ‘your’ comparisons remain **development** records. The row set is frozen before integrated scoring. Together with [L1a](VALIDATION_H_LOSS_V1.md) it supplies 40 reserved records, but some are related grammatical cells, some preserve earlier model conditions rather than discriminate them, and not all priority families are covered.

The original reconstructions were checked in Ringe & Taylor (2014: 173–175, PDF sheets 188–190; same held-source hash as L1a). Published early stages in those pages are not always complete tokens. Where a state is projected, the record names the earlier changes presumed; the output cannot then be used as evidence that the full stack generated the inputs. Exact printed early quantities and normalized outcomes do not furnish independent dialect dates where the grammar gives no manuscript locus. The charter numeral and Psalter participial comparison are separately keyed to Sweet. Sources for the morphology, distinct histories of *iu*, and later variability remain the [earlier audit](../hiatus-cycle3/BACK_VOWELS.md); the same technical setup must not be used to decree an early historical spelling one way or another.

Operational entry is `@proto` at the order's own full admission stage, `@observe` with no machine input, or before an explicit named component, with no stages replayed before that entry. In particular, nuclear *iu/eu* enter the realization comparison already nuclear. This does not presuppose the later identity of `*īo` and `*ēo`. `OEDiphthongLeveling` names the executable comparison boundary, not the historical start date of early OE. `EAFBrighteningUnstressed` is a comparison site for the protected labial inputs, whose earlier endings are independently supplied. The protected ‘threat’ oblique lacks direct attestation at this reconstruction and supplies a **conditional input/output trace**, not an endpoint pass. Positives for historical labial realization, inherited *eu* negatives, and unrelated changes to surrounding consonants must be reported separately.
