# Fourth hiatus research cycle: joint verdict

**CAPR: retain the frozen cascade.** A runnable prospective family now
exists, and the narrow stress, medial *hw*, and post-loss components
are suitable for separate review. The family does not yet justify a
general replacement. **Article: retain the regular short route as a
substantive historical hypothesis.** Its economy is conditional;
the evidence does not establish that Vespasian ⟨heg⟩ was short.

## Final adjudication

1. **What survives the corrections?** The stress-domain diagnosis,
   the strictly post-loss handoff, the admission and realization
   distinction for nuclei, and medial singleton labiovelar reduction
   survive. Like and unlike hiatus are not one empirical class.
   The ‘flock’, ‘receive’, ‘cover’, ‘rug’ and ‘pillow’ results still
   demonstrate their tested paths, but only with their actual
   quantities and input statuses. The matched nonfinal endpoint tie
   and the internally distinct edge outcomes survive.
   [Source-to-input audit](SOURCE_ENTRY_AUDIT.md) · [comparative replay](PROSPECTIVE_STACK.md)
2. **What chronology is withdrawn or reclassified?** The supposed
   short final vowel in ‘flock’ cannot date the introduction of
   heaviness against HVD; long final *ī* stays in both tested orders.
   The prior compilation collision cannot establish incumbent
   atomization in local ‘strew’. Neither the ‘receive’ proxy nor
   the long nuclei of ‘cover’ and ‘rug’ demonstrates short
   identical-vowel coalescence. Earlier exact-input claims for
   Anglian ‘see’ and ‘clay’ are loss results, not full contractions.
   The surviving implications are conditional dependencies, not new
   dated edges. [Ordering ledger](ordering-proposal-v1.tsv)
3. **What is old and what is new?** The grammars already motivate
   intervocalic loss feeding contraction, medial *hw* reduction,
   early identical versus later unlike hiatus, the importance of
   smoothing, and independent palatal histories. Jones's short
   transcription is explicitly fricative and is not the proposed
   derivation from final syllabic *i*. The composite *hewi > he.i > hej*
   has an internal first-cycle precedent; no exact earlier external
   proposal was found in the bounded search. New here are the
   corrected source alignments, isolated matched computations,
   local nuclear-weight contrast, and a sharper conditional
   historical comparison, not discovery of a universal sound law.
   [Priority register](SCHOLARLY_PRIORITY.md)
4. **What belongs in the prospective core?** The S1 implementation
   repair and loss/marker-strip handoff, narrow medial *hw* reduction,
   and independently supported post-loss environments are the
   strongest candidates. The tested starting point is
   `P2-without-back`; the h group itself still needs scope review.
   Do not include the back-vowel arm as implemented, `HbOU`, a
   universal front resolver, or any of the edge policies in a
   replacement proposal. The typed nuclear and stress experiments
   are not yet general representations for the whole cascade.
5. **What discriminates, and what failed?** ‘Lend’ and ‘filter’ supply
   independent medial singleton families, with ‘white’, ‘turn’,
   voiced ‘filtered’ and geminate ‘sieve’ constraining scope. The
   full endpoint of ‘sieve’ and the realization of long *īo* remain
   unresolved. The new ‘flee’ subjunctive and ‘pupil’ direct cell
   isolate the successful broken-nucleus post-loss operation;
   ‘catch’ imperative, uncontracted ‘trough’ and ‘fringe’ separate
   loss, immunity and retention. ‘Toe’ and ‘hostile’ retain a weak
   *æ* where broader contraction would require evidence. The
   corroborated short ‘claw’ probe and protected-front comparisons
   show failures hidden by the wide corpus; the long ‘claw’ path
   also remains unsolved. ‘Thief’ is a proto-admission failure, not
   a phonological loss. Not all reserved families bear on the
   same question, and some contrasts are local or disputed.
6. **Are separate front windows technically necessary?** Not to
   generate the tested nonfinal contraction endpoints on the
   repaired substrate. A strictly late operation reaches both
   post-labial and post-laryngeal inputs, including the diagnosed
   prefixed forms. This does not make one universal operation
   adequate: the same broad arms erase genuine retention and fail
   on exact inputs of different quality. It is an existence
   result, not historical evidence for synchronizing the windows.
7. **Best account of nonfinal chronology?** Start with independent
   adjacency, actual stress, and regional vowel quality. Early
   labial loss can expose hiatus without settling the date of its
   realization. Late singleton loss demonstrably feeds contraction
   where appropriate and can leave hiatus elsewhere. The shared
   endpoints do not distinguish direct contraction, gliding plus
   assimilation, and nuclear incorporation plus monophthongization.
   Direct contraction is the least articulated encoding for the
   supported endpoints, not a historically recovered unique mechanism.
8. **Best account of final short hiatus?** Keep retention, length,
   a short nucleus, and a short vowel plus consonant separate.
   Desyllabification offers a direct, regular route without adding
   a donor operation; no secure independent final short *e.i*
   positive establishes it. The Parker ‘river’ genitive lacks a
   written palatal; if inherited from short *ehi*, it is adverse to
   uniform gliding. Other remodelled ‘river’ cells do not show that
   this oblique was remodelled. The long inherited-palatal control
   *cǣġ/cǣġe* sets a real limit on loss rules, not a precedent for
   creating a palatal from hiatus.
9. **How strong is the Vespasian inference?** The direct accusative,
   positive same-tradition short ‘way’ comparison and regular
   route to *he.i* make short /hej/ an economical possibility
   conditional on early apocope and independently justified final
   consonantal resolution. The integrated fit adds no evidence for
   quantity or ancestry. Farman's acute favours length locally;
   West Saxon high quality provides separate positive evidence
   for considering a protected source there. Neither is a verdict
   on Vespasian. The Canterbury first member, Northumbrian spellings,
   metrically heavy closed syllables and later convergence cannot
   identify the required history.
   [Historical memorandum](SHORT_HAY_MEMORANDUM.md) ·
   [Proposed argument decisions](argument-proposal-v1.tsv)
10. **Next action, separately.** CAPR should review the narrow
    stress correction first, then medial *hw* and post-loss
    contraction with independently evidenced contrasts for long
    *īo*, broken/plain nuclei and exact weak tails. Require
    stage-aware admission and full production adjudication only
    when an adoption proposal is authorized. The article should
    retain the conditional short analysis, correct the source
    quantities and Dahl date in a separately authorized revision,
    and keep the distinction between removing a possible analogical
    cost of early apocope and independently proving that chronology.
    For stronger wording, seek both a matched independent Vespasian
    quantity contrast and a dated final short *e.i* comparandum
    with a secure inherited history: they answer different questions.
    A firmer genealogy of the mutated ‘river’ oblique would sharpen
    the adverse test.
