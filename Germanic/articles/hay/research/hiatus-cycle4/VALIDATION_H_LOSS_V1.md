# L1a — First withheld tranche: h-loss and adjacent vowels

The [versioned panel](validation-h-loss-v1.tsv) freezes 18 particular grammatical records for later scoring, with 12 lexical families or root groups. No candidate stack has been run on this panel. The tranche does not complete the cross-family population requested for L1. Existing benchmark, cycle-two-panel and cycle-three-holdout files stay immutable.

The early and intermediate forms were read in the held Ringe & Taylor (2014) page images, printed pp. 314–316 (PDF sheets 329–331; source SHA-256 `278f94f6076a31278ecca75d8d0429f0822728e5efe0ec3011186a694cf8d6f2`). Edition and witness references are recorded to the level supplied there; these are not new collations of Corpus, Erfurt or Beowulf. Anglian and Kentish written variants are contextual evidence, not automatically alternate West Saxon scoring targets. Quantities in source-normalized forms are distinguished from writing by the actual glossator. Where the source covers a paradigm without a primary locus, the reported form is literature evidence, not an independently measured manuscript quantity.

All rows were reserved as validation at freeze time. ‘Crooked/perversity’, the four catch cells and the two pupil cells are related within their respective families, not independent samples. ‘High’ is an additional grammatical cell in an already used family. The pupil noun is a separate lexeme from ‘see’, but its accusative instantiates the same local input configuration; later reports must not count this as an independent structural result. ‘Sloe’ and ‘joy’ are disputed at the source-entry level and must be reported separately. Predicted trajectories (for example singleton loss followed by later contraction) and written endpoints are different criteria: the intermediate spelling of ‘roe’ shows that treating any uncontracted output as a missed historical h-loss would be wrong.

**Entry semantics.** These are Old English state injections, not Proto-Germanic strings. The source reconstruction supplies earlier nasal loss, fronting/breaking, or morphological restructuring where applicable; they are not rerun. `OEHLoss` is an executable anchor meaning “run from immediately before the singleton deletion network”; it is not evidence for the date of the entry. A form without an executable output must be reported as rejected, not as evidence of deletion or of a false historical derivation. Where the source comparison is prefixed, score only the predeclared stem comparison rather than silently attaching a prefix in output.

**Scoring constraints.** Keep the frozen route, candidate route and secondary eligibility/artifact notes separate. Endpoint matches count only with the intended cell, dialect and trace; no credit merely for matching letters after losing the relevant feature. Dependencies and repeated observations remain visible. If an item later shapes a change, retain this frozen row as previously withheld and add a new independent test. No production corpus addition is made; review of exact input stage, primary loci and existing production inventory must precede any corpus-addition proposal based on these rows.
