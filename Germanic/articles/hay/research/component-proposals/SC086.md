## Diagnosis

Every arm was replayed on all 394 archived and 396 active records
in each order. All P0 surfaces match. All five candidates have empty
firing, intermediate and changed-surface ledgers on those records.
Consequently their interpretation rests on the local contrast traces,
not on a claim of full-corpus attestation. The differences are the
same in every order:

| Candidate | Complete local surface differences and classification |
|---|---|
| `CtBrAe` | *flēoæ > flēo* (‘flee’ subjunctive); *seoæ > sēo* (‘pupil’ direct cell and southern ‘see’ subjunctive, plus the synthetic acute twin). The pupil/see repetition is one structural environment, not independent validation three times. |
| `CtBrE` | *feoe > fēo* in ‘wealth’ dative and its acute twin. This extends an independently motivated **front** environment, but depends on the reconstructed weak vowel. |
| `CtBrA` | *teoan > tēon* on acute ‘draw’. Plain ‘flee’ plural and ‘pupil’ accusative already contract at the incumbent; ‘high’ weak oblique already reaches *hēan*. Do not credit the candidate for those cases. |
| `CtHigh` | *wrīiþi > wrīþi* (‘cover’); *fēiþi > fēþi* (unrounded ‘receive’ proxy); *ēi > ē* (long ‘river’ sensitivity); *lēide > lēde* (lowered ‘betray’ proxy). Historically these are not four demonstrations of the full rule. |
| `CtId` | *teed > tēd*, *siid > sīd* only on acute synthetic twins of identical short contexts. The plain inputs already give *tēd/sīd*. |

The rounded exact ‘receive’ input leaves the relation **at** SC085,
and supplying the same unsupported nucleus **after** it still gives
no final output. Neither is deletion of a historical vowel.
‘Cover’ already has long *ī*; `CtHigh` removes the newly adjacent
short vowel but does not remove independent final *i* from the full
local input. Its erroneous short-root twin is not a historical test
of coalescence. The exact long weak ending of ‘betray’ already gives
*lēde* without the new rule: `OEHighVowelApocope` removes the medial
*i* and the later incumbent weak-ending rules realize final *ǣ*.

Anglian ‘see’ and ‘clay’ with their exact short *æ* leave *seæ*
and *þōæ*. Lowered *e* probes are not replacements for them.
Exact ‘rug’ yields *rȳǣ* and *rȳan*: the front ending and back
sequence need different realization analysis, not a shorter root.
‘Toe’ and ‘hostile’ likewise retain exact *æ* after loss. ‘Catch’
already contracts its infinitive and participle and protects
final *h*. ‘Trough’ and ‘fringe’ retain adjacent back vowels.
The former has a contracted compound comparator worth testing
separately; the latter's retained post-loss ⟨uo/ou⟩ evidence
rules out invoking general back-vowel absorption just for coverage.
The unlike *e.i* and inherited consonantal *j* negatives are unchanged.

## Literature and historical analysis

The [source-entry audit](../hiatus-cycle4/SOURCE_ENTRY_AUDIT.md) and
[quantity audit](../hiatus-cycle4/H_LOSS_QUANTITY.md) pin the
held Ringe & Taylor pages and exact notations. Ringe & Taylor
(2014: 314–318) and Hogg (2011 reprint: §§5.131–135) support
separating actual singleton loss, the affected nucleus and the
weak vowel; Hogg discusses dialectal retention. Ringe & Taylor
315–316 and Hogg §5.134 positively support the southern ‘flee’
and ‘pupil’ comparisons; the Mercian smoothing in ‘see’ predicts
a distinct pre-loss nucleus. Ringe & Taylor 317 prints the long
and rounded roots; 318 compares the different sources of hiatus.
Sweet (1885: 104, 107) and Pheifer (1974: §72(a), 1066) anchor
the ‘fringe’ retention control. Loss feeding contraction is the
existing historical edge; there is no new edge from mere
assay adjacency. No analogy follows from a stage rejection or
from executable invariance.

## Verdict

**RESTRICT** the proposal to independent review of `CtBrAe` and
`CtBrE` first. The exact *æ* contrast has the cleanest source and
ablation support, but both additions still need a genuine
same-environment negative or regional retention contrast before
production. **RETAIN** incumbent plain broken/back and identical
operations; **RETAIN EXPERIMENTALLY** acute parity (`CtBrA`, `CtId`)
as representation cleanup, pending coverage of actual marked paths.
**DEFER** `CtHigh` as a general adoption proposal; the rounded
input, independent tail, and long ‘river’ alternative need
resolution before this broader context can be judged.
No proposed registry edge, input migration, or full SC086 replacement.
