# B1 — Corpus-extension tranche for historical review

The [versioned ledger](corpus-extension-v1.tsv) records 57
grammatical records across 28 independently motivated families.
‘Filter’, ‘strainer’, and their derived forms make one family;
overlapping witnesses of one cell are not new families. The
ledger is a corpus proposal with staged candidates and explicit
holdbacks, **not** 57 new production targets, or a validation-only
panel. It supersedes the earlier candidate list only as a review
queue. No rows enter the frozen capture.

## What is new

- **Actual cells and locators:** the dictionary's simplex headword
  for ‘lend’ is not a verified simplex token; the *on-* past,
  inflected participle and present are different environment
  controls. The dictionary cross-checks for ‘filter’ isolate
  the simplex imperative and the distinct *āsiwen/āseownes*
  forms; the latter cannot be fed by devoicing the voiced
  stem. The ‘strainer’ citation and object *seohhan* are
  distinct from row 2189 *sife*. The early `hh` is a retention
  comparator, not two occurrences of the same word counted as
  two sound changes.
- **Noun-specific ‘pupil’:** Maxims I 123/Wright I.43.1 and
  Deut. 32:10 identify different cells, rather than verbal
  ‘see’ reused as an independent noun. The Kentish gloss
  corroborates the oblique arm but is not a West Saxon target.
  ‘Roe’ supplies the earlier *raha* and h-less *raa*;
  row 2156 lacks an OE selection. The visibly prefixed
  catch/receive cells are not exact matches to the simplex
  root replay.
- **Early and late nuclear witnesses:** distinguish *dioful*
  and *deoful* from the normalized citation *dēofol*, and
  compare ‘friend’ plural, genitive plural and direct singular
  without calling them independent roots. ‘Three’ adds a
  genuinely different grammatical cell, not a change to row
  2254. ‘Tree’, ‘knee’, ‘threat’, ‘few’, ‘straw’ and
  ‘servant’ retain separately identified protected and later
  renewed comparisons; availability of a donor is not proof
  of when levelling occurred.

## Four-way source-to-input standard

| Grade | Meaning for admission |
|---|---|
| `full` | Whole reconstructed grammatical cell and a corresponding attested target. ‘Thief’ is the established full control, not a new selected row. |
| `stage-local` | A source-backed state can enter at the named comparison phase; spellings closer to the left edge can be **intermediate** targets. Local nucleus probes do not claim independent proof of their earlier history. Some do not model later inflectional material; the admission column says so. |
| `disputed` | Rival ancestry, missing material, a source proxy, or unresolved absolute stage. Keep the candidate and actual witness, not a whole-word pass. |
| `observation` | Evidential comparison with no executable cell proposed. If an earlier source stem is recorded it is solely contextual and does not license a full input. |

Use the protected [current aligned TSV](../../../../data/germanic-aligned-final.tsv)
to deduplicate **by actual English cell**, not only by concept
or normalized spelling. Matched existing row IDs are recorded.
When the source gives no marked stress, the prosody field
distinguishes reconstructed root stress, executable assignment,
and unresolved compound or prefix stress. A lexical datum is
not ready for *full* submission merely because a local candidate
in a similar stem fires. The early relative historical stage and
the executable input site remain separate.

## Proposed use

Prioritize local comparison with the noun-specific ‘pupil’,
exact ‘roe’, ‘wealth’, ‘clay’ and ‘fringe’ states and the
replayable ‘strainer’ path. The most useful future independent
holdbacks are **not** previously replayed rows relabelled:
review the newly identified exact *on-* ‘lend’ cells,
*seohhan* with its separately justified weak ending, and the
Kentish pupil accusative after formal stage and input review.
The [SC086 receipt](SC086.md) covers the original local loss
and contraction controls; the early labial and nuclear receipts
are development evidence. No new corpus-wide performance claim
is derived from combining those receipts.

Before any authorized production migration: settle full
admission of the relevant affixes and later states, secure
precise source lines for the remaining edition-index locators,
resolve exact weak vowels without reducing them early for an
endpoint match, and freeze genuinely unseen controls. Omit
unattested normalized predictions as corpus targets. This
proposal neither selects the quantity of ‘hay’ nor changes
any inherited benchmark identity.

The narrow ledger uses Sweet's printed-edition lines and
glossary, not a fresh collation of every original witness;
Toller's numbered online articles 24658, 27502, 27470, 27442
and 32044 anchor the mediated forms. Electronic Sawyer
[S 1482](https://www.esawyer.org.uk/charter/1482.html)
identifies the charter for the late simplex catch comparison;
its sense is assumption of land, not literal physical capture.
