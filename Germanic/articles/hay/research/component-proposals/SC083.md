## Literature

Re-extracted and checked the held Ringe & Taylor (2014: 32, 35, 228)
pages (PDF sheets 47, 50 and 243). The weak source is a suffix
history: shortening and unrounding of the umlauted vowel, then
raising before a palatal and narrower further syncope. Their
*maci(ġ)an* and plural *maci(ġ)aþ* are outcomes of that sequence.
The post-SC082 deletion is a transparent **implementation proxy**;
the scholarship does not posit general literal deletion of *e*
before inherited syllabic *i* in every position. Campbell (1959:
§§754–755) supplies the ‘love’ paradigm and further weak exemplars;
Sweet (1885: Ps 71:9, glossary s.v. *liccian*) supplies Vespasian
*liciað*; the local ‘lick’ experiment is an infinitive, not that
token passed off as an exact input.

For the independently stressed alternatives, the source trail
established by [SC035](../../../../docs/sound_changes/audits/sc035-adjudication.md)
is Campbell (1959: 31 §74 and n. 5), Hogg (1992: 88, 99–100),
and Ringe & Taylor (2014: 334–335): ordinary weak *ge-* contrasts
with stressed *ġeatwe*. Ringe & Taylor (2014: 260, 318) and
Campbell (§237.1b) motivate the comparison with the stressed ‘call’
root; Sweet (1885: 416) supplies the Mercian participial evidence.
None of these sources provides the Foma annotation or a complete
stress grammar for artificially injected stages.

## Historical analysis

Historical scope is English; Ringe and Taylor's early gloss material
anchors the weak development by the eighth century. Conditioning is
weak prosodic position, not morphological class; weak paradigms
supply independently reconstructible inputs. The broad executable
left context licensed a stressed head in the presence of a pretonic
vowel, an out-of-domain firing. Counterexamples to a universal stress
solution remain under decoding and longer pretonic spans.

SC082 > SC083 is a feeding dependency of the model's representation:
the existing displacement evidence concerns true weak forms. The
source historical chain involves an already present *j* and does not
literally date CAPR's intervening `*i` stage. No new independently
demonstrated historical edge, no date for root-hiatus resolution,
and no evidence for analogy follow from correcting this bug.
