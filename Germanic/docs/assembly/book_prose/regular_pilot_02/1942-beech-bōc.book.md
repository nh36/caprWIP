Kroonen's _\*bōk(j)ō-_ already distinguishes nominative _boc_ from oblique _bēce_, so the relevant comparator here is the nominative-singular line represented as _bōc_ [@Kroonen2013, 81]. With _\*bōkō_ as input, the derivation is as compact as the trace suggests: final long _ō_ raises in Northwest Germanic, then high-vowel apocope leaves _bōc_. The prose therefore only needs one reminder: the oblique _bēċe_ belongs to the same paradigm, but not to the form chosen for direct comparison.
