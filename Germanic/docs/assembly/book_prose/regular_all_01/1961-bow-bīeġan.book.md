Kroonen reconstructs the weak causative _\*baugjan-_, and Ringe and Taylor give the fuller development to West Saxon _biegan_ [@Kroonen2013, 75; @RingeTaylor2014, 94]. The verb belongs to the weak causative member of the bend-family. Clark Hall and Bosworth-Toller record the Old English weak verb [@ClarkHall1960, 34; @BosworthToller1898, 102]. The spelling _bīeġan_ used here normalizes that attested form. From pre-Old-English _\*bēagjan_, palatalization of _gj_ and i-mutation yield the West Saxon infinitive.
