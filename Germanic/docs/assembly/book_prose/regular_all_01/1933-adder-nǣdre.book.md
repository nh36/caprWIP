Kroonen distinguishes the masculine snake word _\*nadra-_ from a feminine ablauting formation _\*nēdrōn-_, and gives Old English _nǣdre_, _næddre_ under the latter [@Kroonen2013, 426]. Orel likewise points from the masculine entry to a feminine _\*nēdrōn_ ~ _\*nadrōn_ type [@Orel2003, 325]. The Old English word is securely represented by _nǣdre_, with _næddre_ as a secondary variant. From _\*nḗdrōn_, the stressed long mid vowel develops to Old English _ǣ_, and the weak feminine ending remains as final _-e_, giving _nǣdre_.
