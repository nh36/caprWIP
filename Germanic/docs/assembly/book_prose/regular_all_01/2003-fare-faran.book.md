Kroonen gives the inherited strong verb as _\*faran-_, and Orel gives the same lexeme as _\*faranan_, both with Old English _faran_ among the reflexes [@Kroonen2013, 149; @Orel2003, 132; @Campbell1959, 61]. Clark Hall and Bosworth-Toller distinguish strong _faran_ from weak _færan_, while forms such as _fære_ and _færð_ belong to the present tense [@ClarkHall1960, 113; @BosworthToller1898, 250]. From _\*fáraną_, brightening first gives _\*færaną_; A-restoration before single _r_ returns _\*faraną_, and later reductions yield _faran_.
