# References Index

This repo keeps key linguistic references in `docs/references/`. Check these
first before searching elsewhere.

## Primary sources (consult first)

These are the first-pass authorities on PGmc → OE phonology and morphology.
When investigating a sound change, start here before consulting the broader
list below.

- `docs/references/hogg_vol1.txt` — Hogg (ed.), *The Cambridge History of the English Language*, Vol. 1; cite the individual chapter, e.g. `HoggPhonology1992`, not the separate grammar.
- `docs/references/hogg_fulk_2011_grammar_old_english_vol1_phonology.{pdf,txt}` — Hogg alone, *A Grammar of Old English*, Vol. 1: *Phonology* (1992; held 2011 reprint), `HoggGrammar1992`. Section numbers are stable; §5.48 n.1 is printed p.104 in the held reprint (PDF sheet117), not the original pagination.

## Focused early-text verification

The [SC035 source-critical audit](sound_changes/audits/sc035-source-critical-audit.md)
amends the earlier *gibiodun* transcription explicitly: Sweet1885 p.40
and Pheifer1974 p.6 agree on *gihiodum*. It records the selected original
Épinal and Corpus manuscript images, edition-only limits, and the focused
Seiler2017/Versloot2025 assessment. `Seiler2017` resolves to the open-access
chapter pp.153–174 (PDF sheets162–183); `Versloot2025` retains its stable
key but now records the 2026 issue year and 2025 online-first publication.
Its [collation apparatus](sound_changes/audits/sc035-source-collation.md)
overlays all51 frozen observations without rewriting them, adds the revised
`HerrenEtAl2026` manuscript-column comparison and preserves genuine edition
differences. Exact targeted holdings, revision URLs and hashes are recorded
in the resource index; archetype reconstructions are never counted as
manuscript attestations.

- `Sweet1885`, `docs/references/sweet_1885_oldest_english_texts.{pdf,txt}`:
  complete public-domain EETS83 edition. EE/Corpus pp.39–53,107; early
  Northumbrian pp.125–127,148–149; Vespasian pp.188–189. Edition spelling
  and actual item numbers were checked on page images, not OCR alone.
- `Sweet1871`, `docs/references/sweet_1871_pastoral_care.{pdf,txt}`:
  EETS45, partI, Alfredian Hatton transcription; p.3 and preliminary
  transcription notice checked. Not a claim to have acquired partII.
- `Stevenson1840`, `docs/references/stevenson_1840_durham_ritual.{pdf,txt}`:
  complete Surtees10 edition; p.7 verifies late Northumbrian gi. Public domain;
  repeated scan sheets are not additional manuscript witnesses.
- `LucasLucas2012`, `docs/references/lucas_lucas_2012_epinal_72.pdf`, p.67;
  `Lucas2016`, `docs/references/lucas_2016_corpus_144.pdf`, p.11:
  complete openly accessible manuscript descriptions retained locally.
  Publication years2012/2016, not the2023 digital-posting dates.
  Copyrighted originals are not redistributed. They revise the older
  absolute manuscript dating without dating each inherited gloss.
- `RuneSPforzen`, `docs/references/runes_pforzen_201.xml`: complete RuneS
  find201 descriptive record, https://www.runesdb.de/find/201, accessed
  2026-10-25; CC BY-SA4.0, attributed to RuneS. External date585–610,
  not a date for English raising; translation/segmentation remains limited.
- Existing `Toller1921` supplement holding: p.284, ge-, not the main
  dictionary. The focused SC035 ledger distinguishes exact edition readings,
  related witnesses, normalization and missing access. Acquisition/hashes/
  rights details for all these sources are in `docs/references/README_resources.md`.

## Specialist articles and monographs

### World/prosody intake, 26 October 2026

The [acquisition record](../../docs/references/world_2026-10-26/README.md)
and its holding/page manifests register complete local texts for
`Fulk2010` (pp.126--144), `Fulk2022` (pp.11--18), `Goering2023`
(pp.52--86), `Hogg1996` (pp.3--12) and `Minkova1996` (pp.13--38).
The latter two are independently retrievable contributions in the complete
`Britton1996` volume. Searchable texts are under the Git-ignored
`docs/references/local/world-2026-10-26/`; original PDFs remain local.
Goering is a publisher reflow with print-page anchors, not a facsimile.
Fulk2010's native extraction damages accent/quantity signs; use page images.
Acquisition does not certify critical reading or adopt an author's analysis.
The complete existing `Fulk2018` PDF and full Vision text are also available.
The bounded follow-up registers `Cermak2005`, *Notes on Obscured Compounds
in Old English*,35--45 (complete article read), and `Sauer1992`,
*Nominalkomposita im Frühmittelenglischen* (complete768-sheet holding;
targeted reading68--73,85--92,345--358). Original printed-page maps,
hashes and actual image-check limits are in the same acquisition record.
Their later syncope and inflectional evidence does not itself date SC036.

  clitic status and disputed nominal formations, pp.145--157.

  nominal *ga-* compounds and accentuation, pp.377--391.

  stress, prosodic organization and stacked prefixes, especially pp.22--23,

  prosodic interpretation, especially pp.173--176.

  supplied critical edition, including Roman-numbered introduction, apparatus
  and both indices; §63, p.lxxv is sheet73. The supplied 1998-reprint electronic
  conversion explicitly loses some diacritics and is not a facsimile.
- `Samuels1949`, `samuels_1949_ge_lindisfarne.pdf`: Lindisfarne and Rushworth
  comparison, pp.62--116; direct *gi-* navigation at p.110.

  glossary origins and transmission, pp.17--44; distinguish 1987 publication
  from the 2008 online posting.

  editorial principles, witness readings and archetypes, pp.125--198.

  *Grammatisches*, pp.455--488; broader unstressed-vowel navigation, not
  an SC035 verdict. The whole-volume copy is a duplicate container.
- `Gante2012`, `gante_2012_ga_revisited_{handout,slides}.pdf`: one 2012
  conference contribution, selected for explicit phonological and inherited-
  formation discussion; not a journal article or two independent works.
- `WrightWuelcker1884II`, `anglosaxonoldeng02wriguoft.{pdf,txt}`:
  title-verified second edition, volume II: *Indices*, 1884. Printed pp.53
  and 73 are Latin-index pages, not *gi-mynd* entries; the supplied Toller
  locator requires separate edition/locator resolution, not a fabricated match.

All these PDF names are relative to `docs/references/`. PDFs remain ignored.
Complete restricted searchable texts are local-only in the durable
`docs/references/local/ga-prefix/` holding, not in a disposable cache.
Its restoration bundle has been tested locally; it is not a remote access
service. Only the public-domain van Helten and Wright--Wülcker II complete
texts are redistributed. Bennett's 1968 chapter remains pending.
