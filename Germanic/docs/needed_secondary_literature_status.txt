QUESTIONS TO INVESTIGATE (carry-over):
--------------------------------------

1. Does Fulk's formulation (*i > *e before non-high vowels with coronal
   intervening consonants) make testable predictions?

2. What other scholars propose REGULAR (not sporadic) solutions?
   - Lloyd: systemic analogy (not regular sound change)
   - Cercignani: merger avoidance (explains resistance, not change)
   - Howell & Salmons: place feature hierarchy (explains blocking)
   - Fulk: genuine Neogrammarian rule

3. Can we identify ALL words in our corpus that fall under Fulk's
   environment? *i followed by coronal consonant(s) followed by
   non-high vowel — check which show lowering and which retain *i.

4. Is onset-velar blocking (our novel hypothesis) supported by any
   source? Cercignani says OIc. only, NOT WGmc; our implementation
   assumes it applies to OE.

5. How does *i > *e interact with *u > *o chronologically?
   Our implementation: i-lowering BEFORE u-lowering.
   Is this order attested in the literature?
