[stable-ID crosswalk](pgmc_integration/population_crosswalk.tsv) and
[coverage audit](coverage_audit.md) for current counts and missing commentary.

and the independently certified extended-report route. A missing extended
report is not a missing derivation.

and derivation class are independent. Publication certification belongs to the
existing manifest or the explicitly selected editorial case, not a checklist,
successful output, source-file count or old batch status.

IDs, examine the existing model/ledger and scoped audit evidence, and record
only the new judgement and unresolved premises. Do not restart the completed
alignment survey or the old drafting-lane rollout.
