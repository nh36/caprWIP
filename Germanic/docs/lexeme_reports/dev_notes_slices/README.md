# Development-note history

The complete slice collection, fragment index, orphan fragments and deferred
sound-change material are preserved in [the archive](../archive/dev_notes_slices/).
They retain original arguments and failed hypotheses; their historical `current`
labels do not override today's sources. The stable-ID crosswalk locates row
materials. New lexical research belongs in existing ledgers/memos and the survey,
not a second synchronized slice of current state.
