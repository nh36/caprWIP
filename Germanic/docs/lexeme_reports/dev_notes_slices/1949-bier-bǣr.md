## Development-note summary

The live row is now straightforward in a way the surviving DEV_NOTES material is not. The regular OE comparator is `*bḗrō -> *bǣru -> bǣr`, and the row's current target `bǣr` is the project's normalized OE reflex. The comparative lexeme assignment is supported by Kroonen's `*bērō- f. 'bier'`, with OE `bar, bær`, while the OE dictionaries checked in the packet/memo give the noun under `bær` rather than under the stale project spelling `bēr` [@Kroonen2013, p. 61; @ClarkHall1960, s.v. bær; @BosworthToller1898, s.v. bær].

What survives in `DEV_NOTES.md` for this row is almost entirely **not** a direct note on the corrected OE lexeme. The attached fragments come from the English sandbox's rhotic/breaking work, where modern English `bier` was repeatedly treated as part of a later `/r/`-smoothing problem together with `beard`, `deer`, `spear`, `year`, `bar`, `barrow`, or `birth`. One of those fragments still explicitly grouped `bier` with `*barwōn`, which the live OE row now rejects outright. Those notes therefore belong to project chronology and cross-lexeme English debugging, not to current OE row authority.

Later report prose should keep the contrasts explicit. **Regular comparator for the OE row:** `*bḗrō -> bǣr`. **Current row-target outcome:** `bǣr`, with dictionary-style comparators `bær` and `bar` in the background [@Kroonen2013, p. 61; @ClarkHall1960, s.v. bær; @BosworthToller1898, s.v. bær]. **Superseded project detour:** English-side `bier` notes that try to derive the later modern form through OE breaking, ME smoothing, and post-vocalic `/r/`-loss, sometimes from the wrong lexeme `*barwōn`. None of the surviving DEV_NOTES fragments should now be cited as if they overruled the live TSV's `PROTO = PROTOFORM = *bḗrō`.
