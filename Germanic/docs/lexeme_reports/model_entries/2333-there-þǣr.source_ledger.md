## Attribution and unresolved limits

The eight SC112 audit source units remain additions to the historical
393-row survey, not retroactive products of that systematic pass. This entry
uses the four `there-*-2333` units, their `a-there-*` analyses,
`core-2333` comparison and `r-2333-sc112` rationale unchanged. All five
bibliography keys already exist in `docs/refs.bib`.

The early selected input is CAPR notation, not a verbatim Kroonen, Orel or
Hill reconstruction with an acute. The long Orel alternative is reported
without attributing a particular lengthening date to him. The model's
before-rhotacism placement preserves inherited-r conditioning; the approved
chronology card records no independently demonstrated SC015/SC024 edge.
SC015 does not apply to this a-vowel witness.

The preliminary investigation's rejected Frisian quantity and pagination
claims are not reused. The related `here` problem does not supply a new
selected input or an ordering premise for this entry. Manuscripts, Stiles
and Grønvik have not been newly consulted.
