## Significant human-review issues

1. `2046 grave / grafan` — noun `græf` and verbal `grafan` must remain separate if the entry is later expanded.
2. `2049 guest / ġiest` — normalized West Saxon `ġiest` should remain distinct from Anglian `gest` and from broader dictionary spellings such as `gist` and `gyst`.
3. `2051 hair / hǣr` — stale `*xazwăz` history and the separate `haddr / heordan / hād-` dossier should not be re-imported into this simplex hair entry.
4. `2057 harvest / hierfest` — the entry must continue to distinguish regular native West Saxon `hierfest` from attested lexical `hærfest / herfest`.
5. `2069 hedge / heġġ` — normalized `heġġ` should remain distinct from standard lexicographic `heċġ / hecg`, and the deeper comparative proto question should remain outside compact final prose.
6. `2070 helm / helm` — helmet `helm` must remain distinct from rudder `helma`.
7. `2071 help / helpan` — verbal `helpan` must remain distinct from noun `help`.
8. `2075 hind / hind` — noun `hind` must remain distinct from adverb/preposition `hindan`.
9. `2077 hold / healdan` — West Saxon `healdan` should remain distinct from Anglian and Mercian `haldan`.
10. `2082 horn / horn` — comparative oblique `*xurnăn` must remain subordinate to selected input `*xúrną`.
