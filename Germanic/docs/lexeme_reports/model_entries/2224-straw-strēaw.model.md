### Reconstruction and comparative evidence

Kroonen cites the neuter stem [strawa-]{.iv .recon lang=pgmc sort=strawa role=source_protoform} 'straw' and Old English [`strēa(w)`]{.iv lang=oe sort=streaw role=comparison_form} 'straw' [@Kroonen2013, p. 484]. The selected [stráwą]{.iv .recon lang=pgmc sort=strawa role=selected_input} 'straw' is CAPR's accented nominative/accusative singular, not Kroonen's verbatim full-word quotation. His derivation of the noun from the strew formation concerns genealogical direction; it does not explain the later restoration of the glide [@Kroonen2013, p. 484].

### Old English evidence

The comparison remains attested [`strēaw`]{.iv lang=oe sort=streaw role=target_form} 'straw', not reconstructed [strēa]{.iv .recon lang=oe sort=strea role=regular_output} 'straw'. Campbell discusses restoration of the glide and the compound [`strēaberige`]{.iv lang=oe sort=streaberige role=comparison_form} 'strawberry' [@Campbell1959, pp. 46--47, 232--233]. Brunner explicitly treats the regular free simplex and short obliques as unattested; his paradigm note limits the West Saxon straw evidence to nominative/accusative singular and a doubtful plural. The compound preserves an endingless pattern, not an independently attested free noun or a complete surviving regular paradigm [@SieversBrunner1965, pp. 49, 110, 204--205; @RingeTaylor2014, pp. 173, 387].

### Development to Old English

The regular endingless branch loses the final vowel and vocalizes final [w]{.recon}: [stráwą]{.recon} 'straw' > [stráu]{.recon} 'straw'. The narrow fragment [SC111 PWGmcEndinglessAw](#rule-PWGmcEndinglessAw) combines those operations; its computational holding site does not establish their order against unrelated changes. Its product feeds [SC030 OEAuBrightening](#rule-OEAuBrightening), giving [stráeu]{.recon} 'straw', and [SC032 OEDiphthongLeveling](#rule-OEDiphthongLeveling), giving regular [strēa]{.iv .recon lang=oe sort=strea role=regular_output} 'straw' [@Campbell1959, pp. 46--47; @RingeTaylor2014, pp. 172--173].

Attested [`strēaw`]{.iv lang=oe sort=streaw role=target_form} 'straw' combines that long diphthong with a glide restored from inflected forms. Reciprocally, the long diphthong spreads into the formerly short oblique stem. This mutual levelling explains the long-plus-glide form without inventing a sound law that preserves final singleton [w]{.recon} in the regular endingless branch [@Campbell1959, pp. 232--233; @SieversBrunner1965, pp. 204--205; @RingeTaylor2014, p. 387]. The cascade stops before that levelling: its reconstructed output remains an explicit mismatch, while the attested comparison and selected input are unchanged.
