## Attribution and unresolved limits

The six `filth-*-2335` source units in the pgmc_reconstructions
apparatus are used unchanged; all six bibliography keys already exist
in `docs/refs.bib`. The composite *fūliþō is CAPR's assembly of
Kroonen's base with Bammesberger's suffix type, directly paralleled by
OS fulitha and OHG fülida; it is not printed verbatim by Kroonen, Orel,
Campbell or Bammesberger, and the entry says so. Orel's silence on the
abstract is a verified negative (page image read to the end of the
f-section), not an oversight claim.

The dialect and date of the individual attestations (Aldhelm glosses,
*Liber scintillarum*) have not been newly established beyond Toller's
sigla; no manuscript has been newly consulted. The former phonological endingless-nominative route relied on overgeneral
heavy--light apocope and is withdrawn. Campbell's lār-type account and
Fulk's independent abstract-paradigm evidence remain relevant to the
nonselected nominative; Fulk2010 pp.140–142 also contests the conventional
light-final syncope account. The new accusative avoids that dispute.
The old dental enumeration was a documented architecture proxy; current
SC065 implements bounded general heavy/open high-vowel syncope.

## Accusative selection evidence

Toller1921 p.276 explicitly records *Ðā fylde ādōn of þām Godes temple*
(Hml.S25,378). The noun is the object of *ādōn*, supporting accusative
singular; normalized target *fȳlþe* adds Clark Hall's independently marked
length and the established thorn spelling. The ordinary strong feminine
accusative *-ǭ is the same morphological convention already supported
in the selected soul history, not an invented dative or convenient
short-ai ending. No new manuscript collation is claimed.

The immutable wave6 comparison gives native *fūliθǭ → fȳlþe*.
Original *fūliþō* under corrected conventional apocope/general syncope
gives *fȳlþu*, not the old endingless target. These are exact experimental
outputs, not independent proof of the reconstructed formation.
The old source units, diplomatic quotations, stage judgements and
verification levels remain unchanged.
