### Old English evidence

The Old English noun family is well attested. Orel gives [`tæppa`]{.iv lang=oe sort=taeppa role=target_form}, and Clark
Hall records [`tæppa`]{.iv lang=oe sort=taeppa role=target_form} together with derivatives `tæppere` and `tæppestre`
[@Orel2003, 402; @ClarkHall1960, 305]. The target is therefore a real Old English noun
form, not a reconstructed convenience spelling.
