### Reconstruction and comparative evidence

Kroonen cites the helmet noun as `*helma-` and separately distinguishes a different lexeme `*helman-` 'rudder' [@Kroonen2013]. The selected input [xélmaz]{.recon} ‘helm’ is the nominative-style form used for the helmet noun itself.

### Old English evidence

Clark Hall and Bosworth-Toller record [helm]{.lex lang=oe} 'helm, helmet' as the ordinary Old English noun for 'helmet', while [helma]{.lex lang=oe} 'helm' belongs to a separate rudder lexeme [@ClarkHall1960; @BosworthToller1898, 542].

### Development to Old English

From [xélmaz]{.recon} ‘helm’, loss of final `z` and later loss of the short final vowel yield [helm]{.lex lang=oe} 'helm, helmet'. The development is therefore a straightforward citation-form match.

### Form note

Comparative `*helma-` is headword notation for the helmet cognate set. It should not be confused with Old English [helma]{.lex lang=oe} 'helm', which is a different noun meaning 'helm, rudder' [@Kroonen2013; @ClarkHall1960].
