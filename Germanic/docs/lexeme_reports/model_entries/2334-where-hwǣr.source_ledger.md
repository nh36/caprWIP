## Attribution and unresolved limits

The entry uses `where-kroonen-2334`, `where-orel-2334`,
`where-hill-2334` and `where-rt-2334`, their existing analyses,
`core-2334` and `r-2334-sc112`. Their source status, author commitment
and bounded comparison are unchanged. The historical 393-row survey
is not relabelled a 395-row systematic consultation. The bibliography
keys already exist.

Hill's argument makes a Proto-Germanic date possible; it does not remove
the dependence on his explanation of Scandinavian quantity. Gothic
`ƕar` is not direct proof of shortness. Orel's double citation and
Kroonen's explicit rejection of inherited `*hwēr` must not be merged
into a single authorial opinion.

The Northumbrian `hwār` attributed to Lindisfarne in Bosworth--Toller
is preserved as witness OE04 in the investigation but is not needed
for this entry. It is not equated with Hill's Anglian `hwēr`, and no
Lindisfarne manuscript reading has been independently checked here.
The disputed `here` reconstructions and rejected preliminary Frisian
claims are not premises of this regular a-vowel derivation.
