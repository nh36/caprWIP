# Source extraction ledger — wether / weþer

This is the internal source and selection record for row 2283. The authorized
selection is masculine nominative singular PGmc *weþruz, CAPR *wéθruz, in
both PROTO and PROTOFORM. Orel's *weþraz remains an alternative, not the
selected input. The attested target and regular derivation class are unchanged.
This decision supersedes the earlier survey's unsupported selected *wíθrą;
it does not alter SC015 or assign that rule an adjudication verdict.

The Gothic and Norse lexical evidence is checked in the original comparative

be conflated with neuter veðr 'weather'.

## Selection and publication ownership

The complete nominative *weþruz is explicitly printed by Orel; it is not an
unattributed ending assembled from Kroonen's citation stem. CAPR theta and
root acute are transcription conventions. Source confidence, verification
and historical stage remain independent.

combined book. A concise, regular-class model entry therefore supplies the
requested book note without inventing an assembly mechanism. Its independent
certification in `pgmc_integration/editorial_cases.tsv` also inserts the same
authoritative prose into the complete derivation report. No unrelated entry
is promoted and no scientific class is changed.
