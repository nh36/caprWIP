## Significant human-review issues

1. `2181 shilling / sċilling` — keep the selected modelling input `*skíllingaz` distinct from Kroonen's broader etymological notation `*skellinga- ~ *skillinga-` and `*skeld-linga-`.
2. `2186 show / sċēawian` — preserve the distinction between source-spelled `scēawian` and normalized `sċēawian`, and do not merge the lemma row with finite companion rows `sċēawa` and `sċēawaþ`.
3. `2196 sleep / slǣpan` — keep verbal infinitive `slǣpan` distinct from noun or lookup `slǣp` and from preterite `slēp/slēap`.
4. `2198 smear / smierwan` — keep Kroonen's PGmc `*smerwjan-` distinct from Ringe-Taylor's later-stage `*smirwijana`, and keep WS `smierwan` explicit as the selected target.
5. `2202 span / spannan` — keep verb `spannan` distinct from noun `spann` and noun row `spanne`.
6. `2204 spar / spearra` — keep noun `spearra` distinct from unrelated verb `sperran`, and do not let nearby `spar-` verb dossiers bleed into this noun row.
7. `2217 still / stillan` — keep OE verb `stillan` distinct from adjective `stille`.
8. `2230 summer / sumer` — keep selected `sumer` distinct from common citation form `sumor`, with oblique `sumeres/sumere` preserved as support rather than spun into a full paradigm report.
9. `2232 sunder / sundrian` — keep `sundrian`, `syndrian`, and `sundor` explicitly separate.
10. `2234 swallow / swealwe` — keep bird noun `swealwe` distinct from verb `swelgan`, and treat `swaluwe/swalewan` material as variant or oblique background only.
