# Source extraction ledger — hay / hīeġ

Hecht's edition of the Old English *Dialogues*, p. 36, ll. 2 and 13,
provides the direct mowing context; *heig/hig* and apparatus *heg*
are not the normalized spelling *hīeġ*. *Mid hig* does not witness
the overt protected dative ending. Campbell §120.2 and Ringe–Taylor
pp. 246–250 concern labial conditioning, not proof of an additional
attested inflection.
