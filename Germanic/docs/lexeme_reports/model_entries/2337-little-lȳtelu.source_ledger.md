accusative share the selected strong ending; choosing this shared cell
does not substitute a weak or oblique ending. The attestation is West
Mercian in Fulk's discussion. Quantity is supplied by Fulk and Goering,
not falsely attributed to an autograph macron or Sweet's spelling.
