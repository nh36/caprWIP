# Source ledger — 2099 lick / liccian

Orel (2003, p. 285) gives the comparative weak formation; Ringe and Taylor (2014, p. 50) give the West Germanic and continental comparisons; Bosworth–Toller (p. 614) supplies the infinitival lemma. Sweet (1885, p. 286, Ps. 71:9) prints finite *liciað* for *lingent*. The normalized geminate in *liccian* comes from the infinitival comparison and comparative evidence, not from redoubling the manuscript token. The finite plural is a published comparison, not a second selected row.
