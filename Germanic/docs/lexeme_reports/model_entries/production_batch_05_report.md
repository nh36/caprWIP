## Significant human-review issues

1. `2254 three / þrīe` — dictionary and glossary practice still tends to
   foreground `þrī`, so the selected attested cell `þrīe` should remain visibly
   distinct, and the entry depends on Campbell's paradigm distinction as the
   main control.
2. `2268 wake / wacan` — the entry depends on keeping normalized strong
   `wacan` distinct from directly attested weak `wacian`, as well as from
   Bosworth-Toller's warning about the unattested simplex infinitive.
3. `2274 water / wæter` — the entry still depends on keeping generalized
   `*wátną` distinct from heteroclitic singular `*wátōr`, while preserving
   dialectal `weter` as background rather than as the target.
4. `2284 whale / hwæl` — the entry depends on keeping normalized singular
   `hwæl` distinct from dictionary spelling `hwal`, and the comparative notation
   remains divided between Orel's `*xwalaz` and Kroonen's `*hwali-`.
5. `2286 whine / hwīnan` — the preserved citation reconstruction `*wainōjaną`
   remains a competing comparative label rather than the source of the Old
   English verb, and Seebold's present-tense-only attestation warning should
   stay visible.
6. `2298 wolf / wulf` — the entry remains an unresolved exception and should
   continue to be reviewed as such rather than as a covert paradigm-cell repair.
7. `2302 world / weorold` — the first-element vowel remains genuinely
   review-sensitive because the sources preserve both `*wir-` and lowered
   `*wer-` traditions, and the broader variant cluster must stay distinct from
   the selected form.
8. `2308 youth / ġeoguþ` — the entry depends on keeping the earlier
   etymological headword and the later g-bearing comparative label visibly
   distinct, and later review should preserve the stem-`u` harmony explanation.
9. `2309 make (iptv.2sg) / maca` — the row remains review-sensitive because
   `maca` is a selected finite paradigm form while the ordinary dictionary
   headword is `macian`.
