| Sweet 1885, pp. 245, 609 | Image-checked p. 245 (PDF sheet 259): *geceð* aligned to *invocat*, with *abyssus* as subject. | Kroonen 2013, pp. 278–279, 282 | *kaujan-* with OE *ciegan* and OHG *gi-kewen*; distinct from *kalzōjan-* ‘call, shout’. The full infinitive in the corpus is a normalization, not an exact dictionary quotation. |
| Ringe and Taylor 2014, pp. 206, 247, 260 | Inherited unstressed prefix; simplex West Germanic infinitive with labial gemination; reconstructed prefixed present, weak past, and past participle with singleton *w*. The present is the selected West Germanic input. Its acute and segment conventions are CAPR's. |
| Campbell 1959, p. 100 §237.1b; Ringe and Taylor 2014, pp. 260, 318 | Front-vowel contraction after labial loss, not an application of the weak-vowel proxy. This licenses the normalized long vowel without claiming it is marked in the printed gloss. |
