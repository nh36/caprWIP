## World-specific reassessment, 26 October 2026

The authoritative synthesis is
[`world-2302-adjudication.md`](../../sound_changes/audits/world-2302-adjudication.md);
its structured claim ledger records per-passage verification, not blanket
source certification. PROTO, input, target and classification are unchanged.

- Ringe–Taylor p.34 explicitly allows original *e* beside *i* in the
  simplex; this uncertainty is independent of the selected SC015 application.
  Printed p.202 calls late ON *verǫld* probably borrowed, qualifying use
  of the class comparison at p.326. Both p.202 and p.326 were image-checked.
- Campbell p.244 §610(1) n.2, original PDF sheet260 checked, records
  frequent endingless accusative *weorold*. The old memo's dismissal of
  paradigm-cell investigation is superseded. A conservative ending does
  not alone prove an uninterrupted regular whole-word derivation.
- Fulk2018 pp.154–156,161–162 supplies general ō-/i-stem contrasts,
  not an actual reconstructed full *world* paradigm. Fulk2010 was read
  completely; its conservative-Mercian method informs the analysis without
  transferring neuter *hēafud* cells to feminine *world*.
- Goering2023 pp.79–82 supports a single early-OE LH foot and ordinary
  weak medial behaviour, not an exact PGmc accent or deaccentuation date.
  His actual Beowulf examples are3068a and2343a; prefixed-x controls are
  hypothetical. Reflow sheets21–22 were visually checked, not original
  printed-page facsimiles.
- Hogg1996 pp.3–12 and Minkova1996 pp.13–38 were read completely.
  Their arguments caution against circular prosody/metre inference, without
  denying all secondary accent. Fulk2022 is fully ingested/read but its
  specific soul analysis is reserved for the separate authorised review.
- Complete-cascade i-stem candidates yield `wereald`, not the old
  staged-entry rejection. Earlier ō-stem candidates add SC019 and yield
  `weorold`, but do not date the analogy. Literal composition-vowel
  candidates yield `weorauld`; linking syncope does not resolve that hiatus.
