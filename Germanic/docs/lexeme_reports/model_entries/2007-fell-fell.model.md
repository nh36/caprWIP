### Reconstruction and comparative evidence

Kroonen reconstructs the noun as `*fella-` 'membrane, skin, hide' and cites Old English [fell]{.lex lang=oe} 'skin, fell' beside Dutch [vel]{.lex lang=dutch} 'skin, fell' and German `Fell` [@Kroonen2013]. ### Old English evidence

Clark Hall records [fell]{.lex lang=oe} 'skin, fell' as the noun 'fell, skin, hide', and Bright's glossary likewise gives [fell]{.lex lang=oe} 'skin, fell' with inflected forms such as accusative singular `fel` and dative plural `fellum` [@ClarkHall1960; @BrightCassidyRingler1971]. The target is therefore the attested noun [fell]{.lex lang=oe} 'skin, fell', not the verb `fellan` or the preterite `feoll`.
