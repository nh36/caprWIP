# Source ledger template

Use this ledger before drafting the lexical entry.

- The ledger **may** mention packets, DEV_NOTES, dossiers, traces, and other
  local files.
- The final entry should **not** mention those local files.
- Extract **actual forms**, not vague statements such as “there is
  disagreement.”
- If a source is cited in local materials but no bibliography key exists,
  record that problem here.
- Do **not** invent citation keys.
- Separate **evidence** from **inference**.

## Extraction prompts

For each source, try to record:

- the exact reconstruction or lexical form it gives;
- the stem class, gender, or paradigm value implied by that form;
- the Old English forms actually attested, normalized, or reconstructed;
- the precise claim that matters for the entry;
- whether the claim is direct evidence or a local inference drawn from that
  evidence.

## Common failure modes

- “The handbooks disagree.”
  Replace with the actual forms and who gives them.
- “The source supports the target.”
  Replace with the form that is attested or reconstructed and how it is labeled.
- “This must be analogical.”
  Record the evidence first; inference belongs in a separate note.
