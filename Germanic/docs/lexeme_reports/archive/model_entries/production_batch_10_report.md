## Significant human-review issues

1. `2100 lid / hlid` — keep earlier etymological `*liþuz` separate from selected input `*xlídą`, and do not overstate the vowel explanation as a settled OE sound law.
2. `2102 light / līehtan` — keep the verb distinct from noun/adjective `lēoht/leoht/liht`, and keep WS normalization distinct from Anglian `lihtan`.
3. `2126 milk / meoloc` — preserve the mixed OE dossier `meoloc/meoluc ~ meolc ~ milc`; the selected form is not the only OE headword tradition.
4. `2129 mother / mōder` — keep represented regularized `mōder` distinct from the ordinary transmitted headword `mōdor / modor`, with `mēder` as paradigm evidence.
5. `2138 net / nett` — keep lexical `nett` distinct from merely graphic simplification in `net`.
6. `2141 nightmare / mare` — keep simplex `mare` distinct from unattested `*nihtmare` and from the secondary `mære` headword tradition.
7. `2155 coat / rocc` — keep the garment lexeme separate from the stone word in `stānrocc` and from unrelated `Ruck / ruk`.
8. `2179 sheep / sċēap` — keep West Saxon `sċēap` explicit as the selected target rather than flattening all dialect spellings together.
9. `2093 lead / lǣdan` — do not let noun `lēad` or superseded `*laidjăną` notation re-enter later expansions.
10. `2104 linden / lind` — keep noun `lind` separate from adjectival `linden`.
