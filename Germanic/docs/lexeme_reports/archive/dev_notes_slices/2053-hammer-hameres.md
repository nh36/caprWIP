## Development-note summary

The live row is a deliberate paradigm-cell split, not a silent replacement of the lexeme-level proto. `PROTO` remains PGmc nom.sg. `*xámaraz`, the cognate-set headword reflected across Germanic; row-level `PROTOFORM` is the gen.sg. `*xámaras`; and the OE target is likewise gen.sg. `hameres` [Germanic/data/germanic-aligned-final.tsv:478; Germanic/docs/lexeme_reports/research_memos/2053-hammer-hameres.md:13-18, 49-57]. The packet confirms that this oblique comparator is regular inside the current cascade: `*xámaras` proceeds through Anglo-Frisian brightening and unstressed-vowel merger to `hameres` without extra rescue machinery [Germanic/docs/lexeme_reports/packets/2053-hammer-hameres.md:17-42].

No securely attachable **current row-specific DEV_NOTES dossier** survives beyond shared methodological and regression-control mentions. That absence should be stated plainly rather than hidden. The surviving DEV_NOTES fragments do show that the project now treats hammer as one of the stock precedents for paradigm-cell targeting: the ræst note cites `hammer (*xamaras → hameres, gen.sg.)`, and the later project-precedent summary says rows of this kind should "switch the per-row PROTOFORM to the paradigm cell that yields the attested OE form by regular sound change, leaving the cognate-set headword intact for cross-Gmc inheritance" [DEV_NOTES:line-3208-3210; DEV_NOTES:line-39964-39969]. Those fragments are current and important, but they are methodological witnesses, not a standalone philological argument for row 2053.

The nominative-vs-oblique distinction must stay explicit because DEV_NOTES also preserves evidence for the competing citation-form pathway. After the A-restoration cleanup, DEV_NOTES reports that `hamer` is among the forms now "all correct" [DEV_NOTES:line-3149-3151]. The memo's manual probe gives the same contrast more directly: nom.sg. `*xámaraz -> hamer`, but gen.sg. `*xámaras -> hameres` [Germanic/docs/lexeme_reports/research_memos/2053-hammer-hameres.md:42-46]. Row 2053 therefore does **not** survive because nominative derivation is broken; it survives because the project deliberately wants the gen.sg. paradigm cell, and because exact `hameres` is directly attested.

Philological framing is stronger here than in many other paradigm-cell rows, and the slice should preserve that detail. Exact `hameres` is directly attested in Bosworth-Toller: `Beátendes hameres stipnes tundentis mallei durities` [@BosworthToller1898, s.v. "hamer"]. The citation/headword side is also secure: Orel gives OE `hamor, hamer`, Clark Hall gives `hamor (o¹, e²)`, and the local lexical table records English `hammer` with OE `hamor` [@Orel2003, s.v. "*xamaraz"; @ClarkHall1960, s.v. "hamor"; Germanic/data/old_english_wiktionary.tsv:117]. Brunner's `hamor — hamores` means `hamores` should be treated as an attested competitor/background form, not as a hypothetical straw man [@Brunner1965, §245]. The safest row-specific statement is therefore: citation lemma `hamor` with variant `hamer`; selected row target `hameres`, directly attested and derivationally regular from gen.sg. `*xámaras`; competing `hamores`, also attested in the grammatical tradition but not the form selected for this row.

## Superseded or diagnostic material

The most important diagnostic caution is not an obsolete sound law but an obsolete way of reading the row. Because DEV_NOTES now also validates nominative `hamer`, later prose could easily collapse the distinction and imply that row 2053 is simply a nominative lexeme row with a slightly unusual spelling. That would be wrong. The checked materials require four distinct labels: PGmc lexeme headword `*xámaraz`, row-level comparator `*xámaras`, citation/headword OE `hamor` with variant `hamer`, and selected OE row target `hameres` [Germanic/data/germanic-aligned-final.tsv:478; Germanic/docs/lexeme_reports/research_memos/2053-hammer-hameres.md:42-57, 61-71].

Those materials should remain diagnostic history only and must not override the live row, the packet trace, or the direct attestation of `hameres`.

`hamores` likewise needs controlled handling. It is **not** current row authority, because the live row and packet intentionally select `hameres` [Germanic/data/germanic-aligned-final.tsv:478; Germanic/docs/lexeme_reports/packets/2053-hammer-hameres.md:17-42]. But it is also **not** a false form: the TSV note already flags it, and Brunner's paradigm `hamor — hamores` confirms that the `-o-` oblique belongs to the historical record [Germanic/data/germanic-aligned-final.tsv:478; @Brunner1965, §245]. The form should therefore be carried only as comparator/background evidence unless the row policy is explicitly revisited later.
