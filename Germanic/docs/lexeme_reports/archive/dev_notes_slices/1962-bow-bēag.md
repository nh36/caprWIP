## Development-note summary

The live row is no longer trying to make the OE citation form `būgan` look regular. DEV_NOTES first established that the inherited class-II strong-verb infinitive `*béuganą` would give regular OE `bēogan`, while attested `būgan` belongs to the NWGmc analogical present-system remodelling in which verbs with inherited `*eu` acquired `ū` instead of expected `ēo` [DEV_NOTES:line-14356-14503; @RingeTaylor2014, pp. 39-40; @Campbell1959, §740]. The current row therefore keeps `*béuganą` only as the cognate-set `PROTO`, but uses the singular-preterite paradigm cell `*báug` as row-level `PROTOFORM`, because that cell reaches attested `bēag` by the ordinary class-II preterite development `*au > ēa` [DEV_NOTES:line-43960-43990; @Campbell1959, §136].

The important project chronology is that row 1962 did **not** move directly from analogical infinitive to the present target. An earlier DEV_NOTES pass diagnosed the infinitive mismatch correctly but then solved it by retargeting the row to past plural `*bugun → bugon`, on the grounds that zero-grade cells were Lautgesetzlich whereas the infinitive was not [DEV_NOTES:line-14356-14513]. That repair is now superseded, but it should be preserved because it records the intermediate policy the project actually used in April 2026: the FST was then understood to derive `bugon` regularly from `*búgun`, and the row was updated on that basis [DEV_NOTES:line-14505-14513].

The later widow / stem-`u`-harmony work is what broke that temporary solution. DEV_NOTES explains that once the harmony block from the widow investigation was in place, `*búgun` no longer yielded `bugon`; it yielded regular `bugun`, because `-un > -on` is phonetically blocked for these stems, and the attested `bugon` forms have to be treated as later analogical overlay instead [DEV_NOTES:line-43943-43958]. The lexeme-specific attestation dossier then matters because it shows the practical consequence: handbook and lexicographic sources cite pret. pl. `bugon`, not `bugun`, so the older row had ended up depending on a paradigm form that was structurally regular but philologically insecure for this verb.

The definitive DEV_NOTES retargeting note therefore surveys the paradigm cell by cell and says that two cells are both Lautgesetzlich and well attested: 1/3 sg. pret. `*báug → bēag` and past participle `*búganaz → bogen` [DEV_NOTES:line-43960-43977]. It then prefers `bēag`, not because `bogen` is wrong, but because the singular preterite is the morphological pivot from which the analogical `ū`-present was back-formed, because it requires the fewest cascade steps, and because it is attested in canonical poetic and prose loci [DEV_NOTES:line-43972-43990; @Campbell1959, §740]. That is the row's present working policy: `bēag` is not a workaround, not a reconstruction invented for the dataset, and not the noun `bēag` 'ring'; it is the attested verbal preterite cell that lets the slice keep both philological honesty and clean sound-law mapping.

The short project-status recap near the top of DEV_NOTES is still worth carrying forward because it confirms that this was the final closure state, not an abandoned branch. In the April-30 research-phase summary, DEV_NOTES says the bow/shove rows were retargeted from 3pl preterites with analogical overlay to 1/3 sg. preterites that are "genuinely Lautgesetzlich + universally attested," explicitly citing `*báug → bēag` [DEV_NOTES:line-1455-1464]. For row 1962, that line is the compact statement of current repo policy after the longer investigation had settled.

## Superseded or diagnostic material

The superseded material is the old idea that row 1962 could be stabilized by choosing the 3pl preterite `*búgun → bugon`. That idea was not careless: it correctly separated analogical `būgan` from regular paradigm cells, and it captured a real OE form (`bugon`) attested in late sources. What superseded it is the later recognition that the exact 3pl cell needed by the FST is `bugun`, while the lexeme-specific attested form is `bugon`; once that gap became clear, the row could no longer stay on the plural without mixing regular derivation and analogical overlay. The noun `boga` in row 1963 and the noun-table hit in the packet are also diagnostic-only here: they are co-radical material, not authority for this verbal preterite row.
