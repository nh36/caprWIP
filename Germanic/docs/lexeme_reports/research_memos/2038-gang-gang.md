## Reconstruction and early-stage forms

Three levels need to stay separate:

1. **Cognate-set proto / comparative headword:** the comparative-etymological noun behind the cognate set is PGmc `*gangaz` 'going, journey, way' as reflected in Orel's entry.
2. **Project input form:** TSV `PROTO` and `PROTOFORM` are both `*gángaz`, i.e. the stress-marked form fed into the derivation pipeline.
3. **OE target form:** the row represents OE noun **gang**.

Those levels are compatible, not competing. The project is not choosing between a noun and a verb reconstruction; it is using the noun input `*gángaz` to derive the noun target **gang**. Repo noise around **gangan** belongs to a different lexeme entirely.

The live derivation path is straightforward: final `-z` deletion and later loss of final bare `-a` yield `gang`, with no special NWGmc/PWGmc repair or analogical retargeting required.

## Old English philology

OE **gang** is attested in the repo's lexicographic sources, so this is not a reconstructed-OE case. Clark Hall and Bosworth-Toller both treat **gang** as a noun headword, and Bright's glossary gives **gong (gang)** as a masculine noun meaning 'path, course'.

The main philological distinctions are:
- **attested noun vs. unrelated verb:** noun **gang** must not be confused with verb **gangan** 'to go';
- **citation form vs. inflected forms:** the row's `COUNTERPART` `gang` is the citation/headword form, while Bosworth-Toller shows ordinary inflected forms such as **gange** and plural **gongas**;
- **headword spelling vs. lexical identity:** Bright's `gong (gang)` suggests spelling/headword variation, but not a different lexeme and not a reason to change the row target.

I found no repo-local evidence requiring a special dialect-only or manuscript-only claim for this row.

## Project problem and solution

The project problem is evidence hygiene, not phonological repair. The current row is already a regular derivation, but the packet's supplementary lexical-table hit and several broader repo searches can pull in **gangan** material that belongs to the verb 'go'.

The right project solution is therefore:
- keep row 2038 as a **regular** noun derivation;
- keep `PROTO` / `PROTOFORM` `*gángaz` distinct from the separate verbal lexeme **gangan**;
- use dictionary evidence, not the misleading Wiktionary-table hit, for the noun's OE attestation;
- treat the verbal-diagnostic analysis files as stale or irrelevant project history for this noun row.

## Paradigm probe

A paradigm probe is **not required** for this row. The live citation-form input already yields the intended OE noun, and the row is not being rescued by switching to a different paradigm cell.
