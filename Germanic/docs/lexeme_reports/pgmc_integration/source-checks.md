## Comparability decisions

The adder case is qualified, not settled inter-author opposition; the missing
premise is the short-a feminine's reflex/formation warrant.
Knee's different cells are not a controversy, while its precise inherited
dative ending remains a separate qualified question.
You's same oblique has competing origins, irrespective of regular output.
Thought has a full reconstructed finite word but an unresolved connectorless
formation; Fulk's morphological illustrations are not attributed complete cells.
Hue's e-bearing formation accounts and explicit PGmc i history are consequential,
although endpoint comparability and phonemic detail still require qualification.
Fire has substantive ancestry differences, a non-certified exact selected proxy,
and a separate target-inflection mismatch. The hypothetical restoration of e
is CAPR's interpretation, not demonstrated by the dictionary attestations alone.

Barrow tests both negative categories within restricted scopes. Orel's m./n.
alternatives overlap Kroonen's m./n. presentation; displayed endings alone do
not establish a gender disagreement. Ordinary support for the selected hill
does not close every deeper etymological or sign-interpretation question.

No judgement uses final-output success to choose an ancestry, equates a
bounded alignment with a controversy, or treats comparison tags as an explanation.
These are priority publication cases, not authorization to revise selected
inputs, protected scientific decisions or the production reports.
