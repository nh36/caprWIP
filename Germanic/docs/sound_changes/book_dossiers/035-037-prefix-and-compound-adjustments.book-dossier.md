## 1. Role in the book

This row is a compact derivational seam between the promoted `SC031-SC034`
West Saxon diphthong chain and the promoted `SC039-SC040` medial-vowel core,
followed by the separate scaffold `SC041` final-loss note. Its editorial
problem is not lack of material, but mismatch between source weight and chapter
shape.

SC035 and SC037 are both real enough to deserve explicit prose somewhere in the
assembled half, yet neither looks like the center of a classical handbook
chapter. SC036 is stronger and more source-backed. The book therefore needs to
decide whether to keep the row as one cautious bridge with SC036 in the middle,
or later split around SC036 while keeping SC035 and SC037 as short flanking
notes.

## 3. Traditional description and literature

The literature supports the row unevenly rather than uniformly. For SC035,
the weak English reflex of inherited *ga-* is secure, but the exact Foma
rule is a narrower, compressed representation, not a handbook's single
early *a* > *e* law. Campbell p.31 §74/n.5 and Hogg pp.99–100 support the
stress distinction; Campbell pp.140–141 §§333–334 support unstressed
fronting, and pp.153–155 §§369–370 the later front-vowel merger.
Ringe–Taylor p.206 gives PGmc/PWGmc *ga-*, Gothic *ga-*, OS/OHG *gi-*,
OE *ge-* and OF *e-*; pp.245 and 267 give the believe present/past
derivations, while p.335 explicitly discusses *gi-* > *ge-* and the
uncertain regular-raising versus *bi-*-influence account.
Hogg §3.3.1.3 (p.88) concerns the unstressed-vowel system, not a date
for this exact rule. Campbell p.147 §354 concerns pretonic *i*-loss
in *bi-*/*ni-* and is withdrawn as support for SC035.
These claims were checked in the local text holdings; no new original
page-image verification is asserted
[@Campbell1959, p. 31; @Campbell1959, pp. 140--141, 147, 153--155;
@HoggPhonology1992, pp. 88, 99--100; @RingeTaylor2014, pp. 206, 245, 267, 335].
SC036 is much stronger: Campbell's low-stress
`weorold` / `weoruld` discussion and Ringe and Taylor's comparative `world`
derivation place it squarely inside a real unstressed-vowel corridor
[@Campbell1959, §§338--339; @RingeTaylor2014, §6.3.3].

SC037 is different again. Campbell and Sievers-Brunner give solid evidence for
compound-juncture vowel behavior, reduced-force second elements, and unstable
linking vowels, but that is better read as support for a technical compound
note than for a full ordinary sound-law chapter [@Campbell1959, §§356--357,
367, 386--387; @SieversBrunner1965, §§167--168]. The best current reading is
therefore asymmetrical: SC036 is the source-backed center, while SC035 and
SC037 are the necessary flanks.

## 4. Formal implementation

In CAPR terms the row is best understood as three different kinds of rule kept
adjacent for chronological visibility.

1. **SC035** compresses fronting and later reduced-vowel history in
   selected weak-prefix forms. `*ĕ` is an unstressed technical token,
   not an independently attested phonetic stage or early schwa.
2. **SC036** adjusts medial unstressed vocalism between stronger stress peaks.
3. **SC037** removes or suppresses a linking vowel in compound structure.

That formulation already suggests the book problem. SC036 sounds like something
that can support a chapter center. SC035 and SC037 sound more like left and
right implementation notes that happen to belong in the same local stretch.

SC035 matches initial unaccented `gaCV`, not a morphological boundary.
Current selected389 and migration-aware legacy380 views each contain
one firing: row1944 believe, `*g*a*l*ēa*b*i*j*a*n*ą` >
`*g*ĕ*l*ēa*b*i*j*a*n*ą` > *ġelīefan*. Stressed root controls
gall/guest remain unchanged. The source's *gistīga/gestīga* 'climb into/onto'
is a genuine cluster-prefixed shape excluded by the proxy
[@RingeTaylor2014, p. 335]. Synthetic component controls establish
stress/position restrictions and the absence of boundary recognition;
they are not new historical attestations or corpus admissions.

## 7. Interpretation for the book

1. **SC035** should distinguish the source-backed pre-OE-to-OE prefix

## 12. Follow-up comparison of prefix models

The complete reproducible comparison is in
`audits/sc035-model-comparison/`: all 389 selected rows, the migration-aware
380 identity population and every one of 108 executable stages. A is the
accepted compressed proxy; B permits ordinary weak fronting and later
merger with explicit weak-prefix/root context; C additionally tests the
possible regular pretonic raising after the palatal fricative. Both staged
models preserve every selected final and differ from A at intermediate
stages only for believe. Genuine guest/give/sheep diphthongization survives;
gall is already diphthongized by breaking, not a live ordinary-PD firing.
Begin and stressed bind are unchanged.

The source-backed full comparison `*gastḯganą` uses CAPR's existing
stressed-long-i notation. B/C give *ġestīgan* 'climb into/onto', whereas A
gives incorrect *ġiestīgan*. The cluster exclusion is a real coverage
limit, not a reason to deny the historical prefix or invent a new selected
entry. Suppressing the existing long-i stress notation is a separate
representation control and must not be confused with a historical
shortening of the root.

The historical choice is nevertheless not fixed by that coverage gain.
B does not derive early *gi-*; C derives it by a regular raising which
the source explicitly leaves open against possible *bi-*-influence.
Both can merge independently supplied early weak *gi-* to *ge-*.
The current decision is therefore underdetermination of the precise
pathway, with provisional retention of A for its declared narrow population.
This does not claim A is uniquely required, that a prefix-specific early
sound law has been proven, or that the staged models are impossible.
It avoids making a hypothetical regular law or an exact intermediate
chronology canonical, while documenting the expanded model's real
advantages and the prosodic distinctions a general prefix account needs
[@Campbell1959, pp. 140--142, 153--155;
@RingeTaylor2014, pp. 206, 215--217, 253--255, 334--335].

The earlier historical characterization, A for existence and
technical/runner-limited edge classifications are retained. No literal
rule, selected input, chronology relation, baseline or neighbouring
adjudication changes. The stale composition-site comment is corrected:
the incumbent representation protects against SC107 and subsequent
inappropriate palatal diphthongization, not against stressed SC043.
