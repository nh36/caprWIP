# SC039-SC041: Medial lowering and final-loss bridge

## 1. Role in the book

This row is the local seam between the promoted `SC035-SC037` derivational
bridge and the later brightening/restoration region beginning at SC042-SC043.
Its book function is to keep the turn from low-stress medial vowel behavior
into final-syllable loss explicit without pretending that the handbooks present
one classic three-change chapter in exactly this form.

The internal hierarchy is now clearer than it was in the bare scaffold. SC039
and SC040 form the strongest local pair through reciprocal `widow` evidence.
SC041 is still historically real and book-relevant, but it is broader: its card
reaches back to SC020 and forward to SC046 rather than locking tightly into the
SC039-SC040 core. The chapter-shape question is therefore whether the book
should keep one cautious adjacent bridge with an explicit SC039-SC040 center,
or later split SC041 into its own short final-loss note.

## 2. Name and basic formulation

- **SC039:** OE WI Combinative U Umlaut
  - **FOMA rule:** `OEWICombinativeUUmlaut`
  - **Current role:** narrow one-sided left flank, historically legible through `widow`-type `w`-conditioned low-stress `u`
- **SC040:** OE Med Unstressed U Lowering
  - **FOMA rule:** `OEMedUnstressedULowering`
  - **Current role:** strongest local member, historically anchored by `world` and bounded locally by `widow`
- **SC041:** PWGmc Final Bare A Loss
  - **FOMA rule:** `PWGmcFinalBareALoss`
  - **Current role:** broader final-loss hinge, historically interpretable but less tightly bound to SC039-SC040
- **Combined unit label:** Medial lowering and final-loss bridge
- **Present editorial judgment:** keep grouped as scaffold for now; if later promoted, the most plausible shapes are either a cautious grouped bridge with an explicit SC039-SC040 core or a later split into SC039-SC040 plus a short SC041 note

## 3. Traditional description and literature

The literature supports the row unevenly rather than uniformly. Campbell and
Ringe and Taylor both give direct support for the `widow` material behind SC039
and the low-stress `world` material behind SC040
[@Campbell1959, §218; @Campbell1959, §§338--339; @RingeTaylor2014, p. 322;
@RingeTaylor2014, §6.3.3]. They also keep `youth` inside the same wider
unaccented-vowel and weak-syllable region, even though SC040's rightward
boundary there is broad/far rather than local [@Campbell1959, §332;
@RingeTaylor2014, p. 267].

SC041 has solid support too, but in a broader register. Campbell's treatment of
unaccented final-vowel loss and Ringe and Taylor's explicit discussion of the
loss of word-final short low vowels make the rule historically intelligible as
part of early final-syllable erosion [@Campbell1959, §341;
@RingeTaylor2014, pp. 60--61]. What the literature does **not** give is one
tidy three-part textbook chapter. The source picture instead suggests one tight
SC039-SC040 medial-vocalic core plus one broader SC041 final-loss stage.

## 4. Formal implementation

In CAPR terms the row is best understood as three adjacent rules doing related
but not identical work.

1. **SC039** handles a narrow `w`-conditioned low-stress `u` adjustment.
2. **SC040** regularizes medial unstressed `u` behavior farther across the low-stress system.
3. **SC041** removes a final bare low vowel and thereby turns the cascade toward broader final-syllable loss.

That explicitness is useful because the handbooks more often discuss the region
as a loose historical neighborhood than as three ordered micro-laws. CAPR keeps
the local SC039-SC040 pair and the broader SC041 hinge visible in a way the
book can explain, but it should not flatten them into one perfectly symmetrical
chapter.

## 5. Place in the cascade

`SC039-SC041` follows immediately after the promoted `SC035-SC037` report and
precedes the remaining SC042/SC043 brightening zone. That position makes it a
real architectural bridge even before any promotion decision.

The outward links should remain restrained:

- **Left neighbour:** promoted `SC035-SC037`, especially SC036's low-stress medial-vowel center
- **Immediate right neighbour:** scaffold SC042, then promoted SC043 and SC044-SC045
- **SC040 farther-right cross-reference:** SC072 through `youth`
- **SC041 outward cross-references:** SC020 on the left and promoted SC046 on the right

These are orientation facts, not reasons to build a non-contiguous chapter.

## 6. Order-testing evidence

- SC039 has no positive earlier boundary; the search stops at bundled `PWGmcChanges`.
- SC039 must precede SC040, with `widow`.
- SC040 must follow SC039, reciprocally with `widow`.
- SC040 must precede SC072, with `youth`.
- SC041 must follow SC020, with a broad/far final-`a` failure set such as `beard`, `bosom`, `bottom`, `calf`, and `coat`.
- SC041 must precede SC046, with `craft`, `dale`, `day`, `hazel`, and `mast`.

The practical consequence is that the row contains one tight local claim and
one broader hinge. SC039 and SC040 form the local reciprocal core. SC041 has
real two-sided chronology too, but it does not form the same kind of local
adjacent pair with either neighbor.

## 7. Interpretation for the book

The safest editorial reading is now fairly specific.

1. **SC039-SC040** is the strongest local prose center and can support a real adjacent subsection.
2. **SC041** should remain visible, but as a broader right-hand final-loss hinge rather than as a coequal local partner.
3. **A grouped bridge remains viable** if the book wants one compact seam here.
4. **A later split is also credible** and may ultimately read more cleanly if the project wants SC039-SC040 to stand on their own and SC041 to introduce final-loss material separately.

That means the next decision is no longer about missing evidence. It is about
how much asymmetry the final prose should expose.

## 9. Remaining uncertainty

- cautious grouped bridge versus later split into `SC039-SC040` plus SC041
- how much space SC041 deserves if the grouped row is promoted unchanged
- how explicitly to describe the runner-bounded left edge of SC039 without implying a historical `PWGmcChanges` boundary
- how prominently to mention SC040's broad/far `youth` relation to SC072
- how strongly to frame SC041 as an opening final-loss hinge toward SC046 rather than just as the right edge of one bridge
