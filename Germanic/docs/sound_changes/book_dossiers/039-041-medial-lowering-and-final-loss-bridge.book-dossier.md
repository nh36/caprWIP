# SC039-SC041: Related medial-vowel and final-loss histories

## 1. Current role

This is a grouped research dossier, not a grouped publication source or an
ordering authority. SC039, SC040 and SC041 each have their own registered
reader source and appear at their individual executable positions. The
earlier proposed adjacent bridge, including the alternatives retained in
this dossier's Git history, is superseded by that architecture.

The substantive connection remains: SC039 and SC040 interact through
*widow*, whereas SC041 is a broader final-loss development. Related
conditioning does not make the three entries adjacent.

The scoped SC017 rainbow adoption removes *reġnboga* from SC040's
live applications: its second-root vowel is already *o*. It still
requires grave information through SC037, but no longer dates loss of
that prominence before late lowering. World's adopted weak-u path
remains a genuine SC040 witness. Neither the late rule nor its
conditioning/order changes; *-cund* and later stress computation remain
separate research (`audits/rainbow-2148-adoption.md`).

## 3. Literature

Campbell and Ringe and Taylor support the *widow* material behind SC039
and the low-stress *world* material behind SC040
[@Campbell1959, §218; §§338--339; @RingeTaylor2014, p. 322; §6.3.3].
Youth also belongs to the unaccented-vowel discussion, but that does not
make its old displacement failure a historical chronology demonstration
[@Campbell1959, §332; @RingeTaylor2014, p. 267].

The later lowering and root-u preservation are described in Campbell
[@Campbell1959, pp. 155--156, §373]. The eighth-century unstressed
back-vowel system precedes the later u/o development
[@StauslandJohnsen2015, pp. 21--22]; the preceding long-vowel shortening
and resolution are discussed on pp. 28--31. This is the source-backed
chronology already adopted in `audits/sc021-adjudication.md`, section 9a.

SC041 retains the separate support of Campbell's account of unaccented
final-vowel loss and Ringe and Taylor's discussion of word-final short
low vowels [@Campbell1959, §341; @RingeTaylor2014, pp. 60--61].
The handbooks do not supply a single three-change textbook chapter.

## 4. Current ordering evidence

SC039 precedes SC040: delaying combinative u-umlaut until after lowering gives
*wudowe* instead of *wuduwe*. That dependency does not require adjacent
positions. In the adopted late SC040 stage, *world* undergoes lowering
and *widow* preserves its medial u after root u.
Advancing SC040 no longer supplies the reciprocal widow failure, because
the intervening accepted vowel-resolution sequence restores the medial u;
that displaced model instead gives *weorald* for world. The old reciprocal
measurement remains archived, not presented as a current result.

The old SC040-before-SC072 youth claim is superseded. The original
experiment at `515b0f4b` reproduces *ġeogoþ* when lowering is delayed
after shortening. It also inserts a front glide early, thereby changing
the preceding nucleus. Replacing that retired insertion with accepted
late spelling restores *ġeoguþ* while leaving lowering delayed.
Current SC040/SC072 displacement leaves youth unchanged.

`audits/post-migration-chronology-reconciliation.md` documents the
counterfactuals, source grounds, complete firing census and distinction
between current constraints and archived observations. The original
cards and experiment summaries remain unchanged. The accepted earlier
shortening/later lowering chronology is source-dated, not a reversed
arrow inferred from code.

SC041's separately recorded broad boundary evidence remains: SC020
precedes it on the beard/bosom/bottom/calf/coat set, and it precedes
SC046 on craft/dale/day/hazel/mast. These wider links are not chapter
boundaries. A runner limit for SC039 is likewise not a historical event.
