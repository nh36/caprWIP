# SC005 A-to-u before m — book dossier

## Historical phenomenon

SC005 isolates a narrow pre-`m` raising in unstressed endings, currently represented in the trace by `shoulder` and by the dat.pl.-type morphological evidence discussed in the handbooks.

## Relevant CAPR rule

- `NWGmcAToUBeforeM`
- `*a -> *u` before final `*m` with optional `*i`/`*z`

## Example lexemes

1. `shoulder`

## Source support found so far

1. Campbell discusses `a > o > u before m` in inflectional endings [@Campbell1959, §331(6)].
2. Sievers/Brunner and Fulk also treat the development as part of the wider unstressed-ending history before `m` [@SieversBrunner1965, §44; @Fulk2018, §5.2].
