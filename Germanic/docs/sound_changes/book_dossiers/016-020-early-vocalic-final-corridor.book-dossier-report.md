## 3. How this follows or modifies the SC043/SC063 pilot template

The new dossier follows the pilot logic closely:

1. role in the book
2. literature-first historical framing
3. formal implementation
4. place in the cascade
5. order-testing evidence
6. interpretation for the book
7. neighbouring-change cautions
8. remaining uncertainty
9. a proposed book outline

It modifies the pilot template in two deliberate ways.

1. It replaces the single-change name/basic-formulation opening with a **corridor overview**, because the chapter's main task is to explain why four unevenly supported rules still form one narratable unit.
2. It adds a dedicated **subsection-treatment** section, because the book will need different subsection lengths and different levels of caution across SC016, SC017, SC019, and SC020.
