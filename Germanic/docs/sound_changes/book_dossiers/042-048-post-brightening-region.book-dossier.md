# SC042-SC048 Post-brightening Region

## 1. Role in the book

This region sits immediately after the promoted SC043 Anglo-Frisian Brightening report and immediately before the promoted SC049-SC050 bridge. Its editorial problem is not whether the material matters, but how to keep strict chronology while converting scaffold bands into adjacent production units.

The answer is no longer a single chapter. SC043 already occupies the center of the region, so the old `042-044-045` scaffold cannot be promoted unchanged. The book instead needs a short sequence of adjacent treatments: a narrow SC042 context note, a likely SC044-SC045 pair, and then either a cautious SC046-SC048 corridor or a later split between SC046 and SC047-SC048.

## 2. Hard structural constraint

Grouped production reports may cover adjacent changes only. Non-adjacent links around SC043 must therefore be handled by cross-reference.

That means:

1. `042-044-045` is acceptable as a scaffold context band but not as a final production unit.
2. SC042 must not be bundled non-contiguously with SC044-SC045.
3. SC045's later relation to SC060 and SC048's later relation to SC059 must stay as cross-references rather than chapter-expansion triggers.

## 3. Region-by-region book reading

### SC042

SC042 is a narrow context rule rather than a chapter center. The `rest` derivation gives it a real local window, `SC020 < SC042 < SC043`, but both sides depend on the same witness. In book terms that makes SC042 useful as a brief prefatory note explaining one model-specific left edge of the brightening corridor, not as the center of a larger chapter.

### SC044-SC045

This is the strongest prose-ready pair in the region. SC044 OE Breaking is standard handbook material, and the chronology cards show that it must stand between SC043 and SC045. SC045 OE Velar Fricative Palatalization is narrower in naming, but the local evidence is excellent: the `fee`, `fight`, `flax`, `knight`, and `laugh` set makes `SC044 <-> SC045` the cleanest reciprocal pair in the post-brightening zone.

If one adjacent unit is promoted next, this should probably be it.

### SC046-SC048

This corridor is more mixed but still viable. SC046 has strong historical clarity as restoration/retraction after brightening. SC047 and SC048 are less standard as a named textbook pair, yet they form a broad reciprocal computational core through the large `-en` failure set. Book treatment therefore depends on tone:

- a cautious corridor chapter can keep the whole stretch visible in order;
- a later split could separate the more source-legible SC046 from a tighter SC047-SC048 nasal-tail pair.

The current dossier review does not force that choice yet.

## 4. Source-weight distribution

The sources are not evenly distributed across the region.

1. **Strongest source-backed pair:** SC044-SC045.
2. **Strong standalone source-backed member:** SC046.
3. **Thin but real context note:** SC042.
4. **Most model-shaped material:** SC047-SC048, even though their reciprocal card evidence is strong.

That distribution is the main reason not to treat the entire SC042-SC048 stretch as a single production chapter.

## 5. Recommended eventual chapter sequence

### Option A: the likely path

1. SC042 short context note
2. SC044-SC045 adjacent report
3. SC046-SC048 cautious corridor

This option minimizes fragmentation while keeping all final units adjacent and chronologically clean.

### Option B: the more segmented path

1. SC042 short context note
2. SC044-SC045 adjacent report
3. SC046 singleton or short note
4. SC047-SC048 nasal-tail pair

This option may fit the evidence profile even better, but it should wait until somebody decides that the corridor prose would actually be weaker than a split treatment.

## 6. Cross-references the book should keep

- **Left/internal anchor:** promoted SC043 Anglo-Frisian Brightening
- **Right anchor:** promoted SC049-SC050 bridge
- **SC045 later edge:** SC060 as a cross-reference only
- **SC048 later edge:** SC059 as a cross-reference only

These links matter for orientation, but none of them should pull the book out of strict local order.

## 7. Promotion readiness judgment

The region is now dossier-prepared enough for a human chapter-shape decision.

- **Most ready for promotion after review:** SC044-SC045.
- **Second decision point:** whether SC046-SC048 should stay a cautious corridor or be split later.
- **Least urgent:** SC042, which can remain a short context note until the surrounding units are settled.
