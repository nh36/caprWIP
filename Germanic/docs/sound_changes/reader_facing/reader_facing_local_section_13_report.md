2. `SC017` uses *ġeoc* ‘yoke’ on the left and *nosu* ‘nose’, *sċofl* ‘shovel’, and *sorg* ‘sorrow’ on the right. It places [SC016 OEWsPalatalGlide](#rule-OEWsPalatalGlide) before [SC017 NWGmcULowering](#rule-NWGmcULowering) and [SC017 NWGmcULowering](#rule-NWGmcULowering) before [SC019 NWGmcFinalLongORaising](#rule-NWGmcFinalLongORaising).
3. `SC018` has no positive historical first-break boundary in either tested direction. Both sides are bounded by the search space rather than by a detected historical break.
4. `SC019` uses *nosu* ‘nose’, *sċofl* ‘shovel’, and *sorg* ‘sorrow’ on the left and *ræste* ‘rest’ on the right. It places [SC017 NWGmcULowering](#rule-NWGmcULowering) before [SC019 NWGmcFinalLongORaising](#rule-NWGmcFinalLongORaising) and [SC019 NWGmcFinalLongORaising](#rule-NWGmcFinalLongORaising) before [SC020 PGmcFinalZDeletion](#rule-PGmcFinalZDeletion).
5. `SC020` uses *ræste* ‘rest’ on the left and a broader later set including *befer* ‘beaver’, *cwedu* ‘cud’, and *feld* ‘field’ on the right. It places [SC019 NWGmcFinalLongORaising](#rule-NWGmcFinalLongORaising) before [SC020 PGmcFinalZDeletion](#rule-PGmcFinalZDeletion) and [SC020 PGmcFinalZDeletion](#rule-PGmcFinalZDeletion) before [SC040 OEMedUnstressedULowering](#rule-OEMedUnstressedULowering).
