## Grouped-chapter justification

### `044-045`

Grouped treatment is justified because the chapter explains a close local
interaction:

1. [SC044 OEBreaking](#rule-OEBreaking) creates the broken vocalic outputs.
2. [SC045 OEVelarFricativePalatalization](#rule-OEVelarFricativePalatalization)
   then operates in that already reshaped environment.

### `046-048`

The internal discussion is deliberately split:

1. one historical discussion for
   [SC046 OEARestoration](#rule-OEARestoration), because it is the clearest
   source-backed hinge;
2. one shared historical discussion for
   [SC047 OEHeavySyllableNasalApocope](#rule-OEHeavySyllableNasalApocope) and
   [SC048 OESecondaryNasalization](#rule-OESecondaryNasalization), because their
   derivational interaction and reciprocal \emph{-en} failure set justify
   treating them together.
