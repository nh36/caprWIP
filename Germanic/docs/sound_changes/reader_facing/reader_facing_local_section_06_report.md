## Grouped-chapter justification

### `079-080`

Grouped treatment is justified because the chapter explains a close derivational
interaction:

1. `SC079` removes \emph{*j} after heavy syllables.
2. the resulting derivation can then contain the unwanted final geminate that
   `SC080` simplifies in the `lungen` witness family.

### `081-083`

Grouped treatment is justified because the chapter explains a real local chain
with internal hierarchy:

1. `SC081` prepares the strengthened consonantal stage;
2. `SC082` vocalizes that stage and is the real center of the chain;
3. `SC083` contracts the resulting unstressed \emph{ei}-like sequence.

### `085-086`

Grouped treatment is justified because the interaction is direct:

1. `SC085` removes intervocalic \emph{*h};
2. `SC086` contracts the resulting hiatus.

### `087`

material and chronology do not justify folding it into `085-086`.
