### What the two mappings actually mean

The inherited comments falsely describe a context-free grave-a clause.
Foma's single comma groups mappings under the shared context; independent
parallel rules use `,,`. Formal `test equivalent` confirms that the incumbent
is equivalent to the explicitly conditioned `[a|à] -> u` rewrite (AFR).
It is NOT equivalent to its proposed broader vowel-class variant (AFRV).
Do not infer semantics from the indentation or the prose comment.

Thus both targets require a preceding member of `PGmcStarVowel`, zero or
more intervening consonants, at least one following consonant with no `j`
anywhere in that sequence, and following plain short/long `u`.
The left requirement is not an independently computed stress peak.
Atomic stressed `*ái` is absent from that class; `*ā` is present.
Acute target `*á`, long target `*ā`, absent preceding nucleus, hiatus,
following `i/o`, and right clusters containing `j` do not qualify.
A left-side `j` is not prohibited. This is broader than “the consonant
immediately before u is not j” and narrower than “any preceding vowel”.

## Historical analysis

- Historical stage: OE weak medial vocalism; exact rounding dates unresolved.
  The `oe` label does not turn the early proxy position into a historical date.
- Scope: English-specific account covering dialectally differentiated forms;
  not a claim that every spelling follows the selected WS derivation.
- Conditioning: reduction of non-primary vowels with independently weakened
  prominence is real. Fully retained secondary stress is not that condition.
  The actual high-back context is a bounded executable generalization; its
  entire domain and `j` exclusion are not established as an exceptionless
  historical law by these two etymologies. No grammatical condition is adopted.
- Counterexamples/falsification: the broad retained-secondary interpretation
  is refuted; the actual shared-context network survives the selected corpus,
  but no independent complete historical generality proof is claimed.
  Further weak-vocalism witnesses require reconstructed stress and endings,
  not labels “compound” or “prefix” as hidden conditions.

  reassignment. Their simplex influence on *furlang* and Hogg's transfer
  from inflected compound forms are source-specific analyses, not an
  excuse to declare every inconvenient form analogical. Soul's restored
  final `u` is a separate authorial reconstruction.
- Proxy: historically mixed, not two proven sound laws corresponding to
  the two accent spellings. Preserve identity; do not manufacture successor
