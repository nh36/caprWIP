## Question

Can an independently reconstructed complete lexical history distinguish
the former unrestricted high-linker deletion from the adopted
weight-restricted clause? A successful admission requires an independently
supported compound formation, historical stage, linking-vowel identity,
stress, selected grammatical cell and attested Old English target before
consulting the transducer. Parser acceptance and a desirable output do not
establish those premises.

A retained high vowel after a light first syllable is the principal
discriminating control. Independently supported loss after a heavy first
syllable is a useful complementary positive control, but both formulations
predict that loss: it cannot by itself certify the new restriction.
Intermediate discrimination erased downstream must not be reported as a
final-output regression.
