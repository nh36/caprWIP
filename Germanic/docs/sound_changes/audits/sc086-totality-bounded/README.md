## Independent replay

Extract `evidence.zip` into a temporary directory, then run:

```sh
python3 replay.py .
```

The archive contains its own unchanged existing independent functionality
helper. No Foma, scholarly originals or corpus word-length bound is needed
to replay the stored evidence. The verified result is:

```json
{"complete_paths":18,"functionality_certificates":24,"language_certificates":8,"passed":true}
```

The 24 relation checks comprise 12 positive global certificates and 12 negative
certificates with complete competing accepting paths. Eight regular-language
checks include positive emptiness and negative inclusion witnesses. Eighteen
complete-network path/rejection checks include actual parser acceptance of
epsilon and complete-cascade rejection, as well as both final outputs of the
overlapping-contraction witnesses. A failed inclusion is not a totality proof.

The manifest records every archive payload hash. Actual compiled ATT relations,
full positive certificates, negative arc paths, immutable input snapshots,
native command receipts, complete component traces and candidate source files
are preserved. Native binaries remain in the local immutable experiment
results; their hashes are preserved in the completion receipts, but the
portable capsule uses the complete ATT relations rather than executable bins.

## Experiment status and limitations

`01` remains FAILED. It preserves the unsupported native `test empty`
diagnostic and the undefined HGeneral serialization mistake; neither is used
as evidence. Independently checked valid component exports from that attempt
are retained, not relabelled as a successful overall experiment.

Corrected experiments `02` through `06` completed successfully and were promoted
only after authentic native command receipts and independent evidence replay.
Their `passed` lifecycle outcome means execution succeeded, not that SC086
became functional, totality was established or a historical proposal was adopted.
The native checker false negatives on earlier independently certified
relations remain distinct from the genuine SC086 counterexamples.

`02` supplies typed stress screens, actual cumulative physical99/100,
the whole-token guard, the surviving-gamma candidate, inclusion differences,
and five original/scoped component comparisons. `03` supplies all110 component
traces, full-network agreement, all391 domain memberships and zero391
gamma-candidate differences. `04` preserves source-directed negative probes;
`05` tests32 onset-bearing long/diphthong-head constructions. `06` exports the
actual original/mapped complete networks used to replay final accepting paths.

The stress screens are not fully established historical-domain grammars.
Synthetic witnesses are not attested words. The source-directed *fehiwi*
probe does not reproduce the cited history and is explicitly a negative
finding. The accepted three2254 derivation already contains three adjacent
vowel tags; this is not automatically proof of three historical syllabic nuclei.
No arbitrary contraction preference, parser exclusion, fixed-point iteration,
new analogy, chronological edge or corpus correction is adopted.

The source-image checks described in the reports were made against the held
originals. Copyrighted PDFs, OCR and scholarly page images are deliberately
excluded. Acquisition receipts and edition distinctions remain in the existing
source-holdings inventory. Bennett1968, Stiles1995 and Hock1973 remain unacquired;
independently verified off-machine preservation remains outstanding.
