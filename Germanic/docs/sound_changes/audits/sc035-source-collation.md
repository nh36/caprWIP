# SC035: source-critical collation apparatus

This is the current interpretative overlay to the frozen 51-observation
`sc035-generality/attestations.tsv`, not a replacement historical receipt.
Read its stable IDs, edition locators, independence groups and prosodic
limits together with the corrections here and C01–C07 in
`sc035-source-critical-audit.md`. No reconstructed phoneme, modern accent
or editorial normalization in this apparatus is a manuscript grapheme.

## Witnesses, verification and coordinates

`E` below means a reading verified on an edition image, not an independent
manuscript collation. `M` means the original image was also checked.
Uninspected originals are UNVERIFIED even when two editions agree.
QUALIFIED marks an unresolved letter, correction or historical interpretation.
The modern edition is the revised online *Épinal-Erfurt Glossary*
(`HerrenEtAl2026`), letters A/B/C/P downloaded from the August2026 paths;
its editors page names Herren, Laird, Porter, Sauer and Wieland.
“Sheet” is the PDF sheet within that letter, not a printed page or folio.
Ep/Cp numbers and Ef CGL coordinates are edition identifiers.

Épinal MS72(2) ff.1–14 = bound ff.94–107. Six columns a–f alternate Latin
and interpretation; b/d/f are interpretation columns. For EE76, f.1v/94v,
c/d **line37**, the penultimate of38 physical text lines, has
*adggrediuntur : gi hiodum*. This is a fresh count from the full column,
not the gloss number76 or viewer p7. The following line is
*adfectans : concupiscens*. The h has an ascending stem and right-hand
shoulder comparable with the h-initial glosses near the top of column d;
the b in *bisceredae* three rows above has a closed lower bowl.
The reading h, rather than b, is secure at this image resolution.
Final m is visibly written; no overbar is resolved over it.
Pheifer p.xxiii §4 n.1's erroneous expansion explains transmission, not
an abbreviation newly observed in this image. His p.lxxxiii §72 n.6
apparently-hiatus interpretation does not make h a reconstructed consonant.

Original image URLs, capture hashes and the other exact checked folios
are in the companion audit's manuscript table. Those checks are EE228
f.4v/97v d, EE728 f.9r/102r f, EE207 f.4r/97r f, and Corpus *gibrec*
CCCC144 f.64r, right-hand gloss column. No exact line number is asserted
for those four checks. Original Erfurt and Rushworth images, and original
images for the remaining rows, were not checked. Neither edition agreement
nor an archetype reconstruction fills that gap.

## Toller, counts and source relationships

Toller1921 p.284, ge- (actual PDF sheet296), prints **ga-eádun, Erf.75**,
not OCR *ga-eddun*. Sweet p.40 EE76 and the modern edition's Ef341.4
pair the Erfurt form with the same *adgrediuntur* lemma and Ep/Cp
counterparts. This identifies the referent, not equality of numbering:
Erf75, EE76, Ef341.4 and manuscript f.1v are four different coordinates.
Toller's hyphen/accent are dictionary presentation, not manuscript signs.
The numeral offset must not be applied mechanically to other entries.

Pheifer §63 p.lxxv's40gi/13ge and31gi/34ge, with11bi in each,
remain **published counts**, not a fresh recensus. His separately noted
ti1 versus te2 explains Hogg's larger52i/13e and42i/36e aggregates:
40+11+1=52;31+11=42;34+2=36. This reconciles category arithmetic,
not missing token membership, exclusions or every correction decision.
The nine pairs yield four gi/gi, one ge/ge, two gi/ge and two ge/gi
in both older and modern comparisons. The three related witnesses
and reconstructed archetype must never be counted as independent votes.
Seiler2017 pp.159–164,167–168 supports caution about inherited source
spellings; it does not assign these prefix tokens to specific source batches.

## Acquisition and unverified sources

The four letter PDFs and revised introduction were checked as ordinary
public website downloads, not as OCR authority. The site's copyright is
the editors'; free access is not a redistribution licence. Downloads and
page images remain local-only. The resource index records exact URLs and
SHA256 fingerprints so later revisions cannot silently replace this witness.
The introduction dates the first A–Z completion to2024 and explains
revision against Leiden and *Expositio Notarum*. Those reconstructed
sources are not additional manuscript attestations of the forms above.

Seiler2019, “The spelling of the Erfurt glossary”, pp.525–540, is
bibliographically identified at <https://www.zora.uzh.ch/id/eprint/168922/>.
A reliable readable copy was not obtained (ordinary repository access
returned a challenge/error). Status UNVERIFIED for its argument: no
claim is borrowed from it and no purported gi/ge finding is imputed.
Sweet/Griggs1883, Schlutter1912 and Bischoff et al.1988 were not newly
collated. The modern edition's specific Schlutter report is attributed
as such, not represented as an independently checked facsimile.
These gaps are non-decisive because the key h, final m, gy and Corpus gi
were checked on original images, while remaining uncertain letters are
not used to establish phonemes, stress or chronology.
