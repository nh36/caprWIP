positive evidence dating the English compound.

## Documentary evidence

Crawford1922 p.107 (held PDF129), Genesis9:13–16, has accusative
*minne renbogan*, simplex *min boga*, and nominative *se min renboga*.
The supplied Helsinki Corpus transcription independently exposes these
three contexts. Bosworth–Toller online25654 gives nominative *se rénboga*
and accusative *ðone rénbogan* at *Boutr.Scrd.*21,19–26, and the vocabulary
locators *Wrt.Voc.*i.52,42 and76,33. These are edition/dictionary checks,
not new manuscript collation. Normalized accents do not encode PGmc
stress. None of these spellings distinguishes inherited syncope from
later composition of the already developed words for rain and bow.

## Predictions, before execution

1. Baseline: second-root `ù>ò` at SC017, decoding to *o* before SC046;
   the unstressed linker fronts, retracts at SC046, and syncopates at
   SC114. Post-syncope positional realization retains stop *b* after *n*.
2. SC046-only restriction: linker remains *æ*, becomes *ea* at SC059,
   escapes nonhigh monophthong syncope and gives the previously observed
   *reġneafoga*. Reproduce rather than assume this result.
3. SC059-only restriction: SC046 has already supplied *a*; the restriction
   should not change rainbow's endpoint.
4. Joint restriction: linker remains short unstressed *æ* through SC059,
   deletes at SC114, and leaves postnasal *b*: *reġnboga*.
5. Independent *boga* row1963, *eorþcunde* row2336, stressed restoration
   positives *bake/wade*, and current back-mutation positives
   *heaven/milk/seven/spear/world* must retain their endpoints.
6. Independently staged West Saxon `*ælu`, immediately before SC059,
   must give `*ealu` under the candidate labial/liquid domain. Its /æ/
   is paradigm-supported, not a claimed direct full-cascade PGmc input.
   Dental `*stæðul` is a declared outside-proxy comparison, not a failed
   West Saxon labial-domain positive.
7. Test complete raw-input cascades as well as local probes. SC044
   currently decodes secondary `ǽ/à/è`; therefore first-nucleus anchoring
   alone might misclassify a genuinely subordinate-stressed root.
   A constructed marked/unmarked compound pair will test information
   loss, without being presented as an attested lexical reconstruction.
8. Compare all394 selected rows in each candidate, recording complete
   output sets, every delta, rejection and ambiguity. Match counts cannot
   establish historical conditioning or a compound's formation date.

Any necessary annotation repair must be separated from the two
historical domain restrictions and tested for observational equivalence.
An apparently successful rainbow endpoint cannot license a rule that
fails a required stressed-target control.

## Follow-up preregistration: annotation preservation

The first trial confirms predictions1–6, but its constructed pair reaches
identical inputs after SC044 despite distinct secondary prominence before
it. This is information loss, not evidence for historical deaccentuation.
The first-nucleus-only joint candidate is therefore not ready for adoption.

The next trial will preserve the existing low/front secondary labels from
SC044 through SC059 and decode them there, without inserting a historical
stress-loss event. Consonantal front-vowel classes already admit these
symbols. The intervening secondary nasalization and i-mutation consumers
will explicitly mirror their plain-vowel operations; a high output from
i-mutation can be decoded immediately because neither investigated rule
targets it.

An annotation-only control must reproduce the current outputs for every
selected row. Against that common representation, compare neither
restriction, SC046 alone, SC059 alone and both. The rainbow predictions
remain unchanged. Constructed compounds with the independently established
*bake* and *heaven* root histories (`régna-bàkaną`, `régna-xèbun`) test the
surviving subordinate labels through the complete cascade; they are
notation controls, not proposed lexical admissions or attested compounds.
The marked bake root must still retract, and the marked heaven root must
still back-mutate. Their unmarked counterparts test exclusion. Raw
pretonic `gabákaną/gasébun` test the existing positive-headed-foot
representation, not a new historical assertion about these compounds.

The annotation trial preserves subordinate restoration at SC046 and
subordinate back mutation at SC059, and its annotation-only control is
endpoint-equivalent on394/394 selected rows. It also exposes a later
problem: decoding the restored subordinate *à* immediately after SC059
lets SC114 delete it as if unstressed. Its disappearance at the endpoint
is therefore not evidence against stressed restoration.

The final bounded comparison will retain the same labels through SC114
and decode them only after its unstressed-vowel deletion. This follows
Hogg's independently stated unstressed domain; it is not a proposed
historical date of stress loss. Recheck all394 rows, the four interventions,
the staged West Saxon positive, and the constructed marked/unmarked roots
at SC046, SC059, syncope, positional *b*, and the endpoint. The secondary
bake root must retain its restored short *a* through syncope, whereas the
unmarked counterpart may lose its nonhigh vowel. The secondary heaven
root must retain its back-mutated vowel.
