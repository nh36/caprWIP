| nettle (row 2139) | *nátilōn | netle | existing corpus word | POSITIVE WITNESS for the light-syllable open-medial clause: only selected row where SC066 fires after a light syllable. Kroonen 2013 p. 384 s.v. *natōn- (diminutive *natilōn-). Luick §336 (after Weyhe 1905): deletion of medial *i before *l pre-literary after dentals; Brunner §159c: regular after light syllables, late WS netele an anaptyctic Sekundärvokal; nom. netl attested (CorpGl, Brunner §159 Anm. 3). R/T p. 272 instead treat the change as arrested variation (netle ~ netele). Distinguishes RETAIN vs weight_open_split only on out-of-sample kettle; fires identically under both. |
| spindle (row 2208) | *spénnilō | spinl | existing corpus word | POSITIVE WITNESS (heavy clause) and the sole reciprocal SC066↔SC068 displacement witness: syncope must create *-nnl- before degemination simplifies it (sc068_before_sc066 → spinnl). Historically trisyllabic *spinnilō syncopated while the medial syllable was open (R/T §6.7.3 heavy-syllable law); the post-apocope cascade site sees closed-final *spinnil, so the executable clause is weight-based, not openness-based. |
| thistle (row 2250) | *θístilas (gen.) | þistles | existing corpus word | POSITIVE WITNESS (heavy clause, short V + CC): the selected cell is the genitive, where the medial syllable was historically open. | bottom (row 1959) | *búttmaz | botm | existing corpus word | POSITIVE WITNESS for SC068 independent of SC066: inherited preconsonantal geminate (Kroonen 2013 p. 82 s.v. *budman- ~ *buttman-: "OE botm < *buttma-"; the published model entry cites the same entry by PDF page as Kroonen2013, 120), no syncope feeds it, so it cannot order SC066 against SC068. ALTERNATIVE PUBLISHED DERIVATION: R/T pp. 332, 354 derive botm from *buþm- with WS *þm > tm (ME bothem; "we cannot recover in any detail what happened to these words", citing Weyhe 1905:67–75). CAPR follows Kroonen's geminate variant; the R/T route is recorded as an unresolved published alternative, not refuted. |
| milk (row 2126) | *mélukz | meoloc | existing corpus word | NEGATIVE CONTROL for two generalisations: (1) medial *u next to *l does not syncopate in the selected WS cell (Luick §336.2: *u before *l lost only Nbr ~900, WS only after s/f in the 10th/11th c.; R/T p. 273); (2) Barrack 390 (p. 96) deletes post-liquid *u (*meluk > meolc) — the barrack_390_postliquid variant corrupts this row, so CAPR must not adopt 390 for the selected corpus. Angl. milc/meolc byforms recorded by Hogg §6.20n3. |
| kettle | *kátilaz | cetel (disyll.) | out-of-sample witness | VERIFIED probe (receipt): under the current SC066 the nominative syncopates to a ×cetl-type form; under the adopted reformulation (weight_open_split_ie_short) the medial vowel is retained. The probe reaches the rule as *ʧ*ie*t*i*l and exposed a second defect: `{*ie}` (WS palatal diphthongization of short *e) was classed as a LONG diphthong ("pending probe" in the class comment), so the weight split alone still syncopated it; reclassifying `{*ie}` as short is part of the adopted reformulation (zero corpus delta). Orel 2003 p. 211 s.v. *katilaz (Goth. katils, ON ketill, OE cytel; "an old borrowing from Lat catillus"); R/T p. 272 ("apparently not of cytel, cetel"). The nominative has *i in a word-final closed syllable after apocope and is outside every published formulation of the change — it is NOT a lexical exception. Not admitted: its evidence is fully carried by the probe; an admission would add a Latin-loan selection question without new discriminating power. |
| angel | *ángilaz | engel (disyll.) | out-of-sample witness | LATENT-LIMITATION probe: heavy syllable with *i in a closed final syllable (nom. engel, gen. engles, Campbell §574). Both the current rule and the reformulation syncopate it, because the post-apocope site cannot distinguish *spinnil (historically trisyllabic, syncopated while open) from a nominative that was never trisyllabic. Recorded as an out-of-corpus latent limitation identical in all variants; resolving it requires the paradigm-cell policy already queued for SC067, not a rule change here. |
