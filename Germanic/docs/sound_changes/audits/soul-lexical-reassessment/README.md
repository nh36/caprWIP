### Conditioning and stress

The preferred bounded law deletes short unstressed nonhigh *a/æ/e* in an
open posttonic syllable before a heavy final syllable. It does not depend
on root weight, a case label, a suffix class or word identity. Its
following-heavy definition is the inherited experimental one: consonant
plus long vowel with optional coda, or consonant plus vowel plus coda.
This is a defensible implemented core, not a proof that every historically
possible internal following syllable must be word-final.

K's `RPosttonic` uses the independently established early `FootCtx`
positive-primary context, not SC040's later prominence computation.
It protects the root-domain first vowel and permits arbitrarily many
intervening syllables. SC113 currently marks one pretonic syllable; K
does not provide an arbitrary multi-prefix stress parser.

### Historical analysis and chronology

The source-supported phenomenon is early OE nonhigh open-medial syncope
after both light and heavy roots. Hogg §§6.14–17 distinguishes it from
high-vowel loss and explicitly locates it after umlaut and before
unstressed-*i* lowering. Fulk's conservative light-final contrasts
justify retaining the following-weight question rather than uncritically
adopting J. Luick's longer-word evidence argues against the C/G length
restriction. K is a phonological implemented core of that account.

There are no fresh chronology permutations in this assay. Its insertion
before high-vowel apocope is model-local: the relevant ending quantity is
still visible there. Source-backed chronology after umlaut is independent
support, not a conclusion inferred from a successful generated spelling.
Carrier decoding and model serialization are not historical stage labels.
No new edge is promoted solely from these traces.

Nonhigh versus high syncope have different source-stated conditioning and
chronological behavior. Prefer a separately adjudicated nonhigh phase,
retaining SC065's high-vowel proxy and SC066's later pre-*l* phase.
SC114 was unused at this baseline and is a possible identity only after
checking the then-current registry. This corrects the earlier plan's
suggestion to place early nonhigh deletion and the retained late high
proxy under one serial SC065 identity. The justification for a distinct
identity is the historical distinction, not the convenience of adding
a stage or the word soul. If registry review establishes existing ownership
of this exact nonhigh phase, use that authority instead; do not duplicate it.

6. G's light-root extension is necessary; K removes unsupported total-word

7. Prefer a distinct historically justified nonhigh phase; retain the
   separately adjudicated high-vowel and pre-*l* phases.
8. Yes: *sāwle* and all three directly affected replacement selections have
   defensible regular pairings. No: this does not settle every form in
   their paradigms or all OE syncope/apocope. Adoption can be scoped to

## Native execution, failures and reproducibility

`experiment.py` creates immutable snapshots, compiles in the private
container, and records full candidate traces, sparse transitions for every
input, all selected/original deltas and actual native formal diagnostics.
There are 799 inputs/model: selected391 + original380 + 28 candidates.
Serial traces equal the composed networks for all eight models.
Each intermediate lookup is checked for ambiguity; all final finite
comparisons are unambiguous. Rejected inputs remain empty outputs.

Attempts01 and02 genuinely failed, before complete scientific comparisons.
The productive input grammar permitted short root plus adjacent weak vowel
instead of the intended diphthong. Native-domain priority alone was
insufficient. Attempt03 requires a consonant after short roots, uses native
brace syntax for final *ai*, and checks parsing before expensive compilation.
The native parser's network name `Heavy` is not a historical root-weight
condition: it also parses the light-root hammer input. No candidate was
changed between failures and success. Attempt03's `RPrevious` uses the
corrected grammar; it is not a byte-identical replay of either failed union.
The original failed logs, snapshots and receipts are preserved.

The first focused evidence test also exposed an inherited invalid stage
control: orthographic *h* in `drihtin` is outside the early native alphabet
accepted by `FootCtx`, so K returned rejection, not successful retention.
This result remains in assay03 and is not relabelled a pass. The separate
immutable `soul-lexical-controls-01` uses native pre-orthographic `drixtin`,
replays the exact original kernels and K serialization, and retains it.
All well-formed negative controls pass there; *bedekian* is also retained.
That retention does not prove a general cluster filter outside K's
bounded domain. `control-results.json.gz` records the definition/input
hash linkage and fresh full-input comparator outcomes. Both unsuccessful
focused validation receipts are retained in the certification archive.

All five isolated syncope kernels report native `test functional = 1`.
All eight composed whole models, including literal production, report
