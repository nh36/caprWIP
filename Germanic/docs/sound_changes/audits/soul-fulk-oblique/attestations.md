# Attestation and grammatical-cell inventory

This is a source-critical inventory, not a new concordance. Macronized
dictionary forms are editorial normalizations, not manuscript quantity
marks. Spelling and metrical syllabicity are independent observations.
Composition dates must not be equated with the dates of surviving copies.

## Luke identification

The requested accented quotation is the dictionary's normalized presentation,
not an exact diplomatic transcription. The edition reads in its first
West Saxon column at Luke12:20, p.130:

> hig feccað þine sawle fram þe

Its subject is plural *hig*, verb *feccað*, object *þine sawle*,
and separative phrase *fram þe*. It is not genitive after the possessive:
*þine* is an agreeing possessive, not a preposition requiring a genitive.
Nor is it the dative *minre sawle* at verse19 or the *eowre sawle*
construction at verse22. The Latin parallel is *animam tuam repetunt a te*.

  The first West Saxon column is the Corpus Christi College Cambridge140

  readings collated. Printed p.130 is PDF sheet146 in this public scan,
  inspected directly. No independently inspected manuscript folio is claimed.

Fulk's early syllabicity evidence is therefore stronger than orthography
alone, but the Luke nominative is not itself a metrical control. An
attested later accusative can preserve the regular inherited oblique
span even if nominatives in the same written tradition have been
remodelled.
