# Experimental result and scientific stopping decision

## Result

The accusative is a substantially better regular witness than the selected
nominative. A productive phonological formulation derives inherited
*sáiwalǭ* as *sāwle* without `OESoulProxy`, analogical ending replacement
or epenthesis. It preserves medial vowels before light *-u* and deletes
them before independently heavy endings in the tested controls.

This is a positive research result, not a certified replacement cascade.
The broad A model changes six existing selected outputs; making the
accusative ending's intermediate quality explicit changes ten. Some
comparison forms still have unresolved quality/ending developments.
The correct decision is therefore **DEFER production adoption**, while
identifying accusative singular *sāwle* as the preferred candidate for a
separately authorized migration. The current nominative selection and
every production SOURCE/baseline remain untouched.

## Complete accusative derivation

The historically explicit A sensitivity assay has this actual native trace:

| Step | Form | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Input | *sáiwalǭ | Inherited feminine ō-stem accusative, bimoric nasalized ending |
| *ai* monophthongization | *sāwalǭ | Root long *ā* |
| Final nasal-bimoric unrounding | *sāwalā | Experimental expansion of the ordinary ending development, not a lexical rule |
| Unstressed fronting | *sāwælā | Short nonhigh medial vowel |
| Final-vowel quantity/fronting support | *sāwælǣ | Model represents the still-heavy ending as long front vowel |
| Open-medial syncope | *sāwlǣ | Medial *æ* deleted after heavy initial syllable before heavy following syllable |
| Final shortening | *sāwlæ | Ending becomes short only after syncope |
| Unstressed merger | *sāwle | Final *e* |
| Surface | *sāwle* | Attested accusative |

The source-supported historical account is *-ǭ > late WGmc -ā >
pre-OE -ǣ > -æ > -e* (Ringe–Taylor pp.59, 299–300; Fulk 2018
pp.85–87, 155). The source does not establish the date of every
implementation boundary. In particular SC108 is registered technical
quantity support, explicitly not independent proof of a historical
long-*ā* fronting. Its use above records the machine trace, not a
promotion of its name or position to a newly discovered sound law.
The point supported independently is the still-heavy following vowel
at syncope, not every incidental serialization detail.

The simpler A experiment carries final *ǭ* symbolically until existing
late shortening: *sāwalǭ > sāwlǭ > sāwlæ > sāwle*. It gives the same
accusative but is not a literal claim that nasalization persisted that
late. Both *a* and fronted *æ* are short nonhigh vowels and are deleted
by the same prosodic rule. Early fronting/restoration of the medial
vowel in the retained cascade therefore does not select the historical
analysis by output fit.

Neither derivation contains medial *a > u*, SC040 feeding, final high-vowel
deletion in the accusative, insertion, a case-conditioned law or an
analogy. High-vowel deletion is instead tested on the nominative and
the independent *-u* controls. The accusative's inherited long nasal
vowel is not a high vowel merely because its eventual reflex is *e*.

## Independent controls and limitations

All sixteen component expectations for the A syncope fragment pass:

| Contrast | Native result | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| *sāwalu / sāwalǭ* | retained / *sāwlǭ* | Following light versus heavy, not nominative versus accusative |
| *īdalu / īdalan* | retained / *īdlan* | Independent nonhigh medial vowel |
| *ēadagu / ēadagum* | retained / *ēadgum* | Final *u* alone is not the conditioning; *-um* is heavy |
| *xēaβudu / xēaβudes* | retained / *xēaβdes* | Independent high medial vowel; pre-orthographic serialization |
| *nēatinu / nēatinô* | retained / *nēatnô* | High-vowel contrast after explicitly assumed shortening |
| Closed, long or accented medial near-neighbours | retained | No deletion merely because an input resembles soul |
| Synthetic *tādakǭ* | *tādkǭ* | Non-*wal*, non-*l*, nonlexical positive |
| Light-initial *werudu* | no medial deletion; final *u* lost by A apocope | Independent weight contrast |

Prematurely shortening the final long vowel to short *æ* blocks this
syncope: staged *sāwalæ* finishes as *sāwele*, not *sāwle*. This
falsifies an account that silently assigns the ending's late lightness
to the earlier stage.

Full inherited A controls:

- *ḯdalō > īdelu*: medial and final vowels retained, matching the early
  feminine form.
- *xáubudō > hēafodu*, *xáubudas > hēafdes*: the predicted stem contrast
  survives. The plural has actual *u/u* before SC040; the retained late
  lowering subsequently gives *o/u*. The early *hēafudu* witness is
  not being relabelled as late *hēafodu*.
- *náutīnō > nīetenu*: the full cascade preserves the relevant medial
  vowel and final *-u*. Its West Saxon root vowel is not a claim that the
  Mercian witness was written that way. Medial shortening in inflected
  heavy-ending forms still needs its own correct early serialization:
  staged *nēatinô* is not a complete inherited *nautīnô* derivation.
- *áudagō > ēadegu*, not *eādigu*: the A replacement retains the
  relevant vowel positions but does not establish the later medial
  quality/palatal development. This is an unresolved full-form control,
  not a silently waived success.
- The staged *īdalan* suffix replay gives *īdlen*, because entry after
  earlier secondary nasalization does not encode the final coda-nasal
  vowel's prior history. The component result *īdlan* tests syncope;
  that suffix replay does not certify the complete weak inflection.

Thus the prosodic fragment is genuinely tested beyond soul, but no claim
is made that every word and inflection in the broader Old English
paradigm system has been completely formalized.

The retained-medial A nominative trace is *saiwalō > saiwalu >
sāwalu > sāwælu > sāwelu*, with incidental early fronting/restoration
recorded in the machine trace. No phonological loss of final *-u*
is smuggled in. Fulk's *sāwulu* requires a further source-supported
account of medial quality; this experiment does not invent one. Later
analogy to final-*u*-less feminines and extension of the syncopated
oblique stem remain separate morphological proposals.

B's tested segmental span is *saiwalu > sāwalu > sāwlu > sāwl >
sāwol*. The retained cascade does not implement Ringe–Taylor's
hypothesized earlier West Germanic loss/restoration of nominative *-u*;
their complete account must additionally record that analogical step
outside the phonological transducer. No case-conditioned restoration
was fabricated to make the experiment look purely regular.

The general insertion gives *fugol, tungol, cumbol* and *sāwol*, while
preserving *sāwle* and dental *setl*. Nevertheless it also produces
*felel* from *fell* and *healel* from *heall*. It is not an acceptable
general rule. Its native unrestricted `test functional` returns0,
whereas A syncope and A apocope each return1. Pinned Foma has known
diagnostic false negatives; the insertion's zero is recorded as an
unresolved formal warning, not proof of actual linguistic ambiguity.
Every tested finite evaluation is single-valued and all whole/component
cross-checks pass. No all-domain functionality certificate is claimed
for B, and none is needed to reject its historical overgeneration.

## Cell verdicts and authorization boundary

Accusative: securely attested and independently reconstructed; regular
phonological derivation demonstrated in isolated models under general,
source-supported conditions. Preferred future witness, with adoption
conditional on the outstanding general-cascade adjudications.

Genitive: under Fulk's trimoric *-ôz*, A correctly predicts *sāwla*;
observed *sāwle* needs the independently proposed ending analogy.
Under Ringe's bimoric *-ōz*, A predicts *sāwle* regularly. The
accusative avoids that reconstruction dispute.

Dative: inherited *-ôi* is independently expected to yield *-e*, but the
current CAPR encoding treats it as *ô+i*, and all experimental models
give *sāwelai*. Neither deletion of *i* nor substitution of accusative
*-ǭ* was used to hide this missing diphthong/ending machinery.
It is not a better demonstrated computational witness.

Nominative: early retention is better supported than obligatory early
syncope/epenthesis. Neither inherited medial *u* quality nor all later
variants are fully derived without analogy. Later monosyllabic stem
levelling has positive distributional evidence; final-*u* loss is a
separate, more tentative analogical explanation. No unsupported sound
law is introduced to avoid it.

`OESoulProxy` should not be defended as historical reconstruction.
The accusative research provides a credible way to retire it without
touching world, but an approved migration and controlled SC036/063/065/
066 adjudications, plus the ending/representation questions, remain
necessary. The attached plan specifies those decisions. The genuine
journal stops after a research **DEFER**, before propagation; this is
not falsely recorded as a completed production adjudication.
