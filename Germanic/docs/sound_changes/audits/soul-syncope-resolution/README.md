`*sáiwalǭ → sāwle`. No current implementation is approved for adoption.

SC063/SC065/SC066 pending separate decisions, not the previous broad
replacement of all three.

The new factorial reproduces the minimal result and separates its causes.
The *fundene* counterexample has an incorrect claimed cell and positive
comparative evidence for analogical restoration. *Westene* is not established
by its cited attestation source; its encoded still-long ending is also not
independently established at syncope. Generalizing the fragment to attested
light-root positives additionally changes *hammer*. These are reasons to
adjudicate lexical histories, not to exempt participles or adverbs.
