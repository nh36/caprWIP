## Complete accusative and nominative contrasts

All C–G models derive the separately supplied complete accusative:

`*sáiwalǭ → *sāwalǭ → *sāwælǭ → *sāwalǭ → *sāwlǭ → *sāwlæ → *sāwle → sāwle`.

The respective changes are input admission, `EAFAiMonophthongization`,
`EAFBrighteningUnstressed`, `OEARestoration`, the new nonhigh-containing
syncope, `OEUnstressedLongVowelShortening`, `OEUnstressedAEMerger`,
and removal of technical stars. SC063 does not delete the long ending.
SC040 does not lower a medial vowel; there is none. No insertion or
soul proxy applies. Production's grammar rejects this inherited
accusative; coverage alone and B instead give *sāwele*.

Nominative A/A-coverage gives *sāwol*, B/C/E-high-old-apo/G *sāwel*,
and D/E/F *sāwelu*. The latter tests retention, not correct original
medial quality: it is not Fulk's *sāwulu*. The unresolved quality and
later analogical losses are not automatic objections to the independently
regular accusative. No new nominative repair is proposed.

## What actually happens to high vowels

Nettle: its medial *i* follows a light root and stands before long *ǭ*.
Neither early heavy-root rule deletes it. Retained SC066 does:
`*netilǭ → *netlǭ → netle`. Its failure in F is specifically the omission
of that later light-root operation.

Spindle: production deletes final *u* from `*spinnilu`, then SC066
deletes medial *i*, and degemination gives *spinl*. D/E preserve the
final *u*: SC066 still deletes *i*, but apocope has already passed,
so `*spinnilu → *spinnlu → spinlu`. General early high syncope does
not help, because the following *u* is light. Disabling SC066 adds
the further wrong medial vowel, *spinnelu*. This is not a single
“missing syncope” effect.

Filth: production `*fȳliθu → *fȳliθ → *fȳlθ → fȳlþ`.
D/E instead preserve *u*, and retained SC065 produces
`*fȳliθu → *fȳlθu → fȳlþu`. Disabling SC065 changes the result further
to *fȳleþu*. The inflected *fȳlþu* is real dictionary evidence, not
necessarily an unattested string; it still does not match the currently
selected nominative, nor prove that its ending was phonologically lost.

Fulk2010 §4.6 pp.140–141 supports analogy in the *-iþō* abstracts
through light-root *frymþ, trymþ*, which cannot have regular heavy-root
syncope in any cell. This is positive evidence against treating every
abstract as a direct conservative H–L–L derivation. It does not erase
SC065's umlaut evidence or establish that every heavy-root abstract has
exactly the same analogical history. Preserve the existing adjudication
and its alternatives until separately resolved.

Thistle: E moves the selected genitive's *i*-deletion to the early
heavy-following rule; SC066 then has nothing to delete. Its final
*þistles* remains correct. No change in meaning, target or stage metadata
is implied by that redistributed firing.

The four C/D/E-old-apo/E cells isolate both high-syncope and apocope
effects. Adding earlier high syncope changes intermediate firings but
not selected/original final outputs within either apocope setting.
Changing apocope does change spindle and filth independently. General
high syncope and SC063 replacement are therefore not necessary to
the accusative experiment.

## Controls and bounded SC063 assessment

The original sixteen controls are retained. Twelve additional controls
were source-selected before running. The follow-up corrects one invalid
representation, orthographic *h* in *drihtin*, to admitted pre-orthographic
*x* in *drixtin*. Snapshot01's rejection remains visible; it is not
counted as a phonological negative success. It also adds the documented
*bedecian* limitation, giving 29 distinct control identities.

- Heavy-root nonhigh positives: prince genitive, other genitive, good
  accusative and the explicitly staged bound comparison delete as predicted.
- Light-root *awles*, *fæġnes*: C and early heavy-root E fail to delete;
  G deletes. The root restriction is therefore incomplete for ordinary
  nonhigh syncope. G is a diagnostic expansion, not a complete grammar.
- Closed-medial, long-medial and accented-medial controls retain vowels.
  Corrected final-closed *drixtin* retains *i* under all four kernels.
- Original high-vowel positives for head/cattle are deliberately not
  deleted by a nonhigh-only kernel. Their non-deletion is not a failed
  prediction of that kernel.
- Light-final *u* in soul/idle/head/blessed and independent *ōþeru*
  shapes survives the conservative kernels; *werudu* loses final *u*
  after two light syllables. Long/closed following endings differ from
  prematurely shortened ones.
- *Bedekian* retains its vowels because the tested fragment is bounded
  to three syllables. This does not certify the handbook's proposed
  phonotactic cluster restriction.

The independent ordinary adjective accusatives are full inherited
probes, not staged strings: C–G give *gōdne*, *blindne*, where A/B give
*gōdene*, *blindene*. The complete participial comparison is not solved.
Earlier complete *eādigu* and cattle quantity limitations also remain;
their staged successes were never certifications of the entire histories.

SC063's H–L–L branches conflict with conservative early retention in
Fulk2010 pp.133–139 and Fulk2022 pp.14–17. The source-backed reformulation
question is real, but D/E also mix the changed early apocope with later
legacy syncope. They are not a complete conservative historical model.
C demonstrates that this separate issue need not be solved just to derive
the accusative. It cannot be bypassed if claiming that an entire new
high-vowel cascade represents early Mercian phonology.

## Formal checks, complete traces and reproducibility

All four changed kernels pass native `test functional`. Every whole
model, including literal production, returns native `0`. Global
functionality is therefore not established; neither finite output
uniqueness nor the fact that the baseline also fails is a proof that
this diagnostic is false. No waiver or Foma modification is proposed.

Every composed model agrees exactly with serial component evaluation on
all 784 inputs, with no observed stage ambiguity. This is finite
evidence, not a proof over all accepted strings. Formal results and
stderr are preserved verbatim in `formal`. Do not describe the whole
models as globally functional.

`input_order` contains selected391 first, immutable380 next, then the
thirteen complete probes. Each model records its full stage order and,
for every stage, every changed index with exact before/after sets.
Unchanged indices retain their previous states; rejected states remain
empty. Thus sparse transitions are a lossless representation of every
full intermediate trace. `traces` and `candidate_traces` additionally
expand the principal witnesses. The evidence tests replay every sparse
transition and check it against every final state.

To reproduce, provision the original baseline and private backend as
documented in the parent research, then run `experiment.py --experiment-id
<new-id>`. Do not reuse a completed snapshot identifier or edit SOURCE
while its logged process runs. `control_assay.py --experiment-id <new-id>`
expects snapshot `soul-syncope-resolution-01` and the archived `results.json.gz`;
its input-hash checks intentionally reject nonidentical reuse.
Archived receipts preserve their genuine original staging paths; a local
`staging -> results` compatibility link permits verification without
rewriting any receipt bytes. Native code runs only in the backend.
