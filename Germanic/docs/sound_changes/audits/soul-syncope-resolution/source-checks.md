# Source verification and documentary limits

The preceding soul dossier remains the authority for the completed
accusative attestation, inherited feminine paradigm and Fulk2022 versus
Ringe–Taylor comparison. This continuation does not replace those findings
or claim a new concordance. Original publications, not just CAPR prose,
were used for the focused new questions below. No full copyrighted holding
or source-page image is redistributed in this evidence package.
