## Consequences for the current account

The chapter directly supports the published separation of h, k/g articulation,
later assibilation, daughter-conditioned vowel changes and uncertain shared
inheritance. It strengthens attribution and qualifications, not a new SC052
conditioner or an unconditional chronology edge. It does not discuss Foma,
specify the singleton/geminate/postnasal replacement block, or supply the
key/day delayed-ʝ-merger argument adopted from Ringe–Taylor2014 p.204.

Compared with Chapters3/4 and SC043/044/052, the genuinely competing proposal
is combined fronting with fronted ai and proposed paradigm leveling. That
requires its own scientific assessment before changing the accepted working
account. No direct contradiction forcing a production edit was established.
