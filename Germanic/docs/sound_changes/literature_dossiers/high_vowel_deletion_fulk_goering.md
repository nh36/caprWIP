## Historical analysis

Fulk--Goering is preferred for early English high-vowel deletion, with
conservative Mercian paradigms as the discriminating evidence.
HLL contains two viable feet; in HLH the intervening light syllable cannot
join either heavy foot. Initial LL/LH resolution licenses the root foot.
The phonological condition is quantity/prosody, not grammatical membership,
lexical diffusion or optional application.

Medial shortening < HVD < final shortening is independently argued from
nēten quantities and metrical evidence (Goering2023 pp.67--72).
Mutation < HVD remains independently motivated by lost triggers and the
selected filth root. HVD < SC068 is a feeding constraint in the retained-nn
spindle oblique: *spinnilǭ → spinnlǭ → spinlǭ → spinle*.
It is not independent proof that nn survived to HVD. The single-n oblique
retains i at HVD and reaches spinle through later SC066 instead.
Technical annotation decoding is not a historical destressing date.
No new registry chronology edges are adopted from these experiments.

The compact core works on the source-backed HLL/HLH contrasts without new
stress machinery. Nevertheless it is not a certified whole account:
the later n-loss proxy contradicts *nētīn*, the fee selection depends on
an unsupported direct u-stem deletion, and the spindle nominative and
conservative pre-l histories are not independently resolved.
Plain long vowels also do not universally encode absence of secondary
stress. Arbitrary compound-long-vowel behavior was not certified by these
thirty stage probes or by selected-population functionality.

## Verdict

Prefer Fulk--Goering historically; defer production adoption.
The conventional model is not vindicated by its higher score.
The required source-backed nēten singular fails the unchanged full suffix,
and there is no warrant for masking this with an accent/ending trick or
an unreviewed global n-loss repair. The fee evidence positively favors early
stem transfer rather than a compensating post-h high-vowel exception.
These specific existing-tail/selected-history contradictions invoke the
assignment's stopping rule; a new prosodic architecture was neither built
nor shown necessary.
