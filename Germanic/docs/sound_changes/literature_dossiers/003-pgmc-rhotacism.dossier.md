# SC003 PGmc Rhotacism — literature dossier

## Historical phenomenon

This dossier concerns the change of medial `*z` to `*r`, represented in the inventory by `deer`, `hoard`, `learn`, `berry`, and `learn (3sg)`.

## CAPR rule

- change_id: `SC003`
- display_name: `Rhotacism`
- rule_name: `PGmcRhotacism`
- FOMA definition: `*z -> *r || EnglishStarVocalic _ ?`

## Example lexemes

1. `deer`
2. `hoard`
3. `learn`
4. `berry`
5. `learn (3sg)`

## Source support found so far

1. Hogg states that Germanic `/z/` yielded `/r/` in intervocalic position in Old English, though it was generally lost finally [@Hogg1992, p. 37].
2. Ringe and Taylor discuss rhotacism explicitly and argue that it was not uniform in West Germanic and occurred independently in Norse and West Germanic [@RingeTaylor2014, p. 98].
3. Crist distinguishes final `*z` deletion from rhotacism and makes the relative chronology explicit [@Crist2002, pp. 1, 4].
