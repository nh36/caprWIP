## Literature and historical analysis

[Cell sources](cell-sources.md) record dictionary pages, the long-vowel paradigm and actual tokens; [archived labial trials](source_integration/README.md) distinguish FWA, FNS, FNG and FII from the matched assumptions. Hogg's discussion of *hyll/hyse* (1979:68–73), Hogg–Fulk §§2.19–2.25, 3.41, and Adamczyk (2018:148–151) support the distinction between available alternants and demonstrable direction of transfer. Continental witnesses are scoped in [the *peti/bedi* collation](morphology/peti.md).

The proposed sound changes concern phonetic sequences and vowel quantity in West Germanic and its English continuation, not grammatical categories. The postvocalic eligible non-*r* consonant doubles before consonantal *j* when a following vowel survives. In the alternative early nasal history, the newly final glide instead becomes syllabic. Independently supported controls include heavy *rīċe/ende*, *r*-exempt *here*, protected *strew*, and *sǣ* for noneligible hiatus. None resolves the relative date of the short vowels. The short singleton and preserved geminate labial routes differ in more than chronological order; they are conditional witnesses. No stage is inferred from the name of an operation.

Positive evidence for transfer is strongest in *searu : searwes*: the donor has the consonantal cluster required for breaking, the recipient lacks it and retains its own syllabic ending. *Searu* does not prove that transfer happened in *nett*. For glee, *gliwes*, *glige/gligge* and *gleo* document stem variation, not a verified textbook *glīġ : glīowes* opposition or the direction of leveling. *Mete : mettas* has better paired attestation but belongs to an i-stem subclass; *hyse : hysses* is conditional on a contested stem-class assignment and uncertain transmission. The protected donor is attested for hue, available by derivation but not case-secure for hay, and attested without proof of spread for the classic light neuters.

Chronology: A's SC047-before-SC010 and B's inverse are experimental hypotheses, **not independently demonstrated**. SC041-before-SC010 is held constant here, not newly adjudicated. Ringe–Taylor's documented PGmc *-ōi* to PWGmc *-ē* before later English shortening is a **stage-entailed** dependency within their historical account, not a new SC047 edge. Early SC020 before SC019 for the experimental *rest* genitive, SC112 before experimental nasal loss, and delayed labial vocalization are executable dependencies only.
