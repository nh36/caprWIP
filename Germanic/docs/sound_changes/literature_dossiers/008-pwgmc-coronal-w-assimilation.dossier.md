# SC008 Coronal-w assimilation — literature dossier

## Historical phenomenon

This dossier concerns the assimilation of coronal sequences before `*w`, yielding `*ww`, especially in `four` and in plural-pronominal forms such as `you` and `your`.

## CAPR rule

- change_id: `SC008`
- display_name: `PWGmc Coronal W Assimilation`
- rule_name: `PWGmcCoronalWAssimilation`
- FOMA definition: `*d -> *w / _ *w` and `*z -> *w / _ *w`

## Example lexemes

1. `four`

## Source support found so far

1. Ringe and Taylor describe `*zw` and `*dw` assimilating to `*ww` as a shared Proto-West-Germanic innovation [@RingeTaylor2014, pp. 56--57].
2. Their historical examples are `four`, `you (dat.pl.)`, and `your (pl.)` [@RingeTaylor2014, pp. 56--57].

## Source gaps

1. The historical evidence is compelling but concentrated in very few examples.
2. The present compact trace foregrounds `four`, while the pronominal support remains source-facing rather than trace-heavy.
