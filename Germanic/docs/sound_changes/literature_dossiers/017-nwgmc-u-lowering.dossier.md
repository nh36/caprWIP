# SC017: Northwest Germanic u-lowering

## Identification and law

`PNWGmcULowering` lowers the stressed first nucleus *u/ú* to *o/ó*
before a following non-high vowel, with its declared nasal/j exclusions.
The late orthographic SC016 is not its phonological feeder.
Campbell1959 p.43 §115 and Fulk2018 p.56 establish the familiar
lowering and regular yoke/gold/participle examples. RingeTaylor2014
p.129 reconstructs the lowered yoke paradigm.

## Conditioning and the technical repair

The immediately post-target consonant in the literal matcher excludes
nasals; the entire consonantal interval excludes *j*. Initial *j*
does not block yoke. Fulk2018 p.56 distinguishes tautosyllabic and
heterosyllabic nasals; do not silently identify these source conditions
with a blanket exclusion of every intervener that happens to be nasal.

Nasal bimoric *ǭ* is a non-high conditioner. Ringe2017 pp.242–243
explicitly reconstructs wool nom.*wullō and acc.*wullǭ. The seven
general input/long/back/vocalic/non-high class repairs admit the latter
without changing any rewrite body or composition. Oral *ō*, nasal *ǭ*
and trimoric *ô* remain distinct; SC019 raises only the first.
The complete direct/transitive consumer census and 53 component checks
are in the new technical receipt; all387 selected rows preserve every

## Exceptions and paradigm history

Campbell1959 p.43 §115 and Bülbring1902 pp.45–46 §116 preserve the
high-root exceptions. Their original-page images were not newly
certified; the investigation's text-verification limitation remains.
Luick's relevant discussion is §§77–79, printed pp.104–106, especially
§78 on pp.105–106, not “§§105–106.” He rejects a labial protection
rule with regular low-root counterexamples and discusses alternating
paradigms, including wool/love and an inferred shovel doublet.
CAPR does not replace these difficulties by lexical diffusion,
grammatical conditioning or a special exception branch.

Rust's selected *rústaz, alternative *rustō and regular *rost remain
the separately adopted source-backed account (Orel2003 p.309;
Ringe2017 pp.247–248). The high target is not a reason to exempt -st-.

## Relative chronology: adopted qualification

Retain SC017 → SC018 → SC019 provisionally. Fulk2018 p.56 argues
from *skulō → scolu for lowering before final oral raising.
The selected nose/shovel/sorrow low roots follow without further
oblique-o generalizations. Nose already involves earlier remodelling
(Kroonen2013 p.383); Luick's shovel doublet makes alternation credible,
not its later direction (1921 pp.105–106). These are conditional
comparisons, not pristine independent proofs.

Stiles2012 p.45 explicitly states the opposite order. With correct
nasal recognition, earlier raising regularly produces wool nom.*wullu
and low-root acc.*wollǭ; subsequent nominative-u generalization is
RingeTaylor2014 p.32's plausible but unproved explanation. Their
pp.33–34 and Stiles2012 pp.47–48 independently support early analogy
in general, not this specific operation.

CAPR's qualitative economy preference is not a numerical match
criterion, a proof against RT, or a novelty claim. The reciprocal
SC017/019 measured displacement records survive as computational
observations. SC019/020's independently established relation is not
weakened. The main comparative account, Runic dating and predecessor
search belong to the SC019 dossier/reader and new adoption memo.
