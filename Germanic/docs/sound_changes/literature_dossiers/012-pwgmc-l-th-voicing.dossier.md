# SC012 Word-internal lþ-voicing — literature dossier

## Historical phenomenon

This dossier concerns the development of word-internal `*lþ` to `*ld`.

## CAPR rule

- change_id: `SC012`
- display_name: `PWGmc L Th Voicing`
- rule_name: `PWGmcLThVoicing`
- FOMA definition: `*θ -> *d || *l _`

## Example lexemes

1. `field`
2. `fold`

## Source support found so far

1. Ringe and Taylor say that word-internal `*lþ` became `*ld` by regular sound change in northern WGmc and give examples such as `fealdan`, `beald`, `wuldor`, and `gylden` [@RingeTaylor2014, pp. 170--171].
2. Campbell likewise states that medial `lþ` became `ld` in West Germanic and cites `fealdan`, `wuldor`, `beald`, `gold`, and `feld` [@Campbell1959, §414].
