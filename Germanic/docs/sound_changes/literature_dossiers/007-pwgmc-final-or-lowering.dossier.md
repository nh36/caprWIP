# SC007 PWGmc final *ō-lowering before *r — literature dossier

## Historical phenomenon

This dossier concerns the lowering of final bimoric `*ō` to `*a` before word-final `*r`, especially in the `four` and `water` families.

## CAPR rule

- change_id: `SC007`
- display_name: `PWGmc Final Or Lowering`
- rule_name: `PWGmcFinalOrLowering`
- FOMA definition: `*ō -> *a || _ *r .#.`

## Example lexemes

1. `four`
2. `water`

## Source support found so far

1. Ringe and Taylor state that surviving bimoric long `ō` became PWGmc `a` word-finally and before word-final `r`, with `four` and `water` as key examples [@RingeTaylor2014, pp. 58--59].
2. Fulk gives the same broad historical outcome for `ō` before final `r` in West Germanic [@Fulk2018, §5.3].
