# SC010 West Germanic j-gemination — literature dossier

## Historical phenomenon

This dossier concerns West Germanic consonant gemination before `*j` after a short vowel.

## CAPR rule

- change_id: `SC010`
- display_name: `PWGmc J Gemination`
- rule_name: `PWGmcJGemination`
- FOMA definition: consonant doubling before `*j` after a short vowel

## Example lexemes

1. `hedge`
2. `net`
3. `set`
4. `sit`
5. `will`

## Source support found so far

1. Fulk states that before `j` the change regularly applies to any consonant other than `r` after a short vowel [@Fulk2018, §6.15].
2. His examples include OE *scieppan*, *settan*, *lecgan*, *fremman*, *wennan*, and *sellan* [@Fulk2018, §6.15].

## Source gaps

1. The current pass did not gather an equally explicit second source to complement Fulk's statement.
2. The present dossier therefore rests primarily on one strong handbook formulation plus the trace evidence.
