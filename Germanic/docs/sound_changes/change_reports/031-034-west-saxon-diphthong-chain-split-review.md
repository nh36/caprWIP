# SC031-SC034 West Saxon diphthong-chain split review

## Current scaffold problem

The current `SC031-SC034` scaffold is useful for visibility, but it should not
yet be promoted unchanged. `SC031` and `SC034` form a real local reciprocal
pair, with `dew` and `hew` showing that the two rules constrain one another
directly. `SC032` also has a genuine two-sided profile, but it is awkwardly
shaped: its earlier boundary across `SC030` is carried by a large cluster of
no-output failures rather than by ordinary alternate surface forms, while its
later `head` boundary across `SC040` is historically real but not part of the
same tight reciprocal core. `SC033` has a real later historical boundary across
`SC044`, but that right-hand relation is broad and far rather than a local
adjacency claim.

The earlier sides of `SC031` and `SC033` also need careful handling. In the
default chronology cards, both earlier breaks only reach bundled
`PWGmcChanges`, so they are not ordinary historical first-break constraints.
Each card has a separate expanded-PWGmc supplementary note (`SC031` across
`SC011`, `SC033` across `SC008`), but those supplementary notes must not
replace the default bundled-profile evidence. The result is a plausible
four-change West Saxon diphthong zone, but not yet a clearly settled book
chapter shape.

## Possible split options

### Option A: Keep SC031-SC034 as one scaffold only

This remains useful for visibility. It keeps the whole West Saxon diphthong
zone assembled in one place and avoids premature chaptering decisions. It is
probably too mixed for immediate production prose, however, because the four
cards do not all support the same kind of narrative weight.

### Option B: Prepare SC031-SC034 as one full West Saxon diphthong-chain report

This is historically attractive if the literature treats the whole sequence as
a coherent chain. The risk is that the chapter becomes too diffuse: `SC031` and
`SC034` form a tight local pair, but `SC032` is awkwardly no-output-heavy and
`SC033` points rightward toward `SC044` with a broad/far boundary rather than a
local reciprocal one.

### Option C: Promote SC031-SC034 as two paired subreports later

A possible shape would be `SC031`/`SC034` as a WW/AW simplification and
long-diphthong pair, with `SC032`/`SC033` treated as leveling and EW residual
material. This has one real attraction: it foregrounds the strongest local pair
without forcing singleton chapters. The weakness is that the second pair looks
partly artificial, since `SC032` and `SC033` do not define one another by a
reciprocal chronology relation.

### Option D: Prepare SC031-SC034 literature dossier first, then decide

This is the safest option if the structural review alone cannot choose a stable
subunit. A dossier can ask whether the source tradition treats these rules as a
single West Saxon diphthong chain, as several smaller phenomena, or as a mixed
zone whose best prose organization differs from the current scaffold.

### Option E: Prepare SC031-SC034 around the SC031-SC034 reciprocal pair first

This foregrounds the strongest local evidence, especially `dew` and `hew`, and
could produce the cleanest eventual narrow report. The risk is distortion:
`SC032` and `SC033` do belong to the same general diphthongal region, so
promoting the reciprocal pair first might make the surrounding material look
more residual than it really is.

### Option F: Leave SC033 for a breaking/context note

Because `SC033` has its later historical boundary across `SC044`, it is worth
asking whether it belongs better with the later breaking context around
`SC043`/`SC044` than inside a `031-034` report. That possibility should stay
open, but the current evidence is not strong enough to justify moving `SC033`
yet. Its broader diphthongal setting still points back toward the present
scaffold.

## Recommended next action

The best next task is a literature and book dossier for the whole
`SC031-SC034` region, not a promotion of the current four-change unit. The
structural review shows that the region is real, but it does not yet settle the
best chapter shape. That source work should ask directly whether the handbook
tradition treats these four rules as one West Saxon diphthong chain or as
several smaller phenomena.

Until that dossier exists, the current four-change scaffold should stay in
place. The later prose architecture may still split around the strong
`SC031`/`SC034` reciprocal pair, but that should be decided only after the
literature has been reviewed. In that next phase, `SC032`'s no-output failures
should be handled explicitly as failed derivations rather than as ordinary
alternate reflexes, and `SC033`'s expanded-PWGmc note plus its broad/far
boundary across `SC044` should be treated cautiously rather than overread as a
tight default chronology claim.
