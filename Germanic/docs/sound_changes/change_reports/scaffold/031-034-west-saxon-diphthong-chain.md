Saxon diphthong chain. The aim is to keep the local corridor visible now
without pretending that the final prose architecture is settled.

so the current entry remains a scaffold placeholder.

`OEAwLongDiphthong`. It follows the glide/fronting entry and leads into the
prefix and compound adjustments.

and `SC033` keep the chain from collapsing into a single two-change note. The
result is stronger than a miscellaneous cluster but still short of finished

#### Status

literature dossiers and human judgement about whether the eventual book keeps
the full four-change chain together or promotes one narrower subsection first.
