Knob etymology resolution — email from Guus Kroonen (2026-01-22)

Summary:
- Old English *knob* is unattested; first attestation is Middle English (Chaucer).
- The likely OE form would be *cnobba*, but it is not attested.
- Frisian has knobbe and Faroese has knubbi with similar meanings.
- These forms point to a Germanic weak noun *knubban-.
- OE cnæp belongs to *knapp-, not the knob (*knubb-) family.

Quoted line from Guus (re: Chaucer):
“The knobbes sittynge on his chekes.”
