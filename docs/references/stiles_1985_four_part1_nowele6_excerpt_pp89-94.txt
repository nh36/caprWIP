KEY FINDINGS FOR SC008 AUDIT
=============================
1. STILES'S OWN NOTATION:
   - *dw cluster: "Go. fidwor" → WGmc *fewwar / *feuwar (§1.3.2)
   - *zw cluster (§1.3.3): "Go. acc., dat. izwis" → "OE eow, OFr. iu, OS iu, OHG iu"
                            "Go. gen. izwara" → "OE eower, OFr. iuwer, OS euwar, OHG iuwer"
     NOTE: Stiles prints "izwara" (not "izweraz" as in R&T); R&T's form "izweraz" is a
     different reconstruction of the same Gothic word; both refer to the same Gothic form.

2. STILES'S CHARACTERIZATION OF THE *zw EVIDENCE:
   "the parallel development of the sequence *-zw-, which is generally acknowledged
   to yield WGmc. *-ww- in the oblique cases of the second person plural pronoun"
   — §1.3.3 (journal p. 92)
   This is one pronoun with multiple case forms, not two independent etyma.

3. *dw COUNTER-EXAMPLES (§1.3.3, pp. 92–94):
   Stiles explicitly lists: *gaiðwa- 'lack', *skaðwa- 'shade', possibly *[k]wiðwa- 'cud';
   *mēðwo- 'meadow', *baðwo- 'battle'. He discusses why each probably or possibly
   did not undergo the assimilation. His explicit conclusion (p. 94):
   "I must confess, therefore, that I am not wholly sure how these exceptions are
   to be explained, though I am sure that the development posited for '4' is phonological."

4. DEGREE OF CERTAINTY: Stiles is confident *-ðw- > *-ww- is phonological (for '4')
   but explicitly acknowledges the wa/wo-stem counterexamples are NOT fully resolved.
   The overall change is "generally acknowledged" and the basic parallel is sound,
   but the exceptional nouns remain "tentatively" explained.
