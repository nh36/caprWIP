# Dresher1985: local holding and pagination

The supplied item is an EPUB of Bezalel E. Dresher, *Old English and the
Theory of Phonology*, not a PDF of the first printing. The preserved
original title and copyright identify Garland Publishing, 1985,
*Outstanding Dissertations in Linguistics*, ISBN 0-8240-5425-3, based on
the 1978 University of Massachusetts dissertation. The added Routledge
copyright page says “This edition first published in 2019”; the EPUB
package date is 2018-07-28. The download's 2018 filename is therefore
not substituted for either the original year or the reissue imprint.
The reissue's electronic ISBN is 978-0-429-45531-5.

The unchanged supplied EPUB is held privately at
`docs/references/local/dresher-1985/original.epub`, SHA-256
`37fb47a80d190b71145b92bffb247a7d3b9761c65526cdb0c6dde10a35af0049`.
`holdings.tsv` records both original and searchable-text hashes.
No redistribution permission was established. The EPUB, complete
extract, unpacked XHTML and source images stay in the already ignored
local holdings directory; none is a public attachment.

`prepare_holding.py <supplied.epub>` preserves the original and makes
`complete.txt` from the package's declared reading order. Embedded
image-only formulae and paradigms are represented by explicit image
paths, not silently omitted or purportedly transcribed. This is a
navigation aid, not a diplomatic edition.

`page_map.tsv` maps the retained `pg_` anchors to EPUB members, including
associated image members. Printed 205–254 are in `OPS/chapter04.xhtml`:
205 maps to `#pg_205`, 217 to `#pg_217`, 229 to `#pg_229`, and 244 to
`#pg_244`. There is no PDF-sheet offset. Image filenames are not folios:
for example, `f0236-02.jpg` follows the retained page-237 anchor.
The map preserves that distinction.
