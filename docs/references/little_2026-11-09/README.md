## Search aids, rights and provenance

`holdings.tsv` uses the existing acquisition schema. Complete texts are
private under `docs/references/local/little-2026-11-09/`; `pdftotext -layout`
was used except for the separately identified Russian OCR. The SHA256
of each original, chosen text and retained native text is recorded.
`page_map.tsv` records inspected pagination anchors, not fictitious
coverage or an original/reissue concordance.

All PDFs, complete extracts, OCR and page images remain ignored/private.
No redistribution permission has been established or asserted.
Only bibliographic metadata, provenance, short analytical paraphrases
and consultation records are committed. Preserve the originals and
the manifest together in a local backup; a public clone alone cannot
restore the copyrighted holdings.

No source is known only through a ChatGPT summary. The ten requested
works have now been consulted directly in the editions identified
above; the1982 edition qualification remains explicit. Thurneysen1917,
Matzel's original studies, and the EWA volumeV alternative are consulted
only through their named intermediaries, not falsely recorded as
new original holdings. Remote etymologies remain proposals, not
implemented rules or changes to the selected lexical cell.
